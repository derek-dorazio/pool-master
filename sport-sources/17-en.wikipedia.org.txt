The '''2026 Belmont Stakes''' was the 158th running of the [[Belmont Stakes]] and took place on June 6, 2026, at [[Saratoga Race Course]]. The post time for the 2026 “Run for the Carnations” was at 7:01 p.m. [[Eastern time zone|ET]], 3 minutes earlier due to impending weather.
This was the third time in as many years that the last leg of the [[Triple Crown of Thoroughbred Racing (United States)|Triple Crown]] would take place at Saratoga due to ongoing construction at the race's usual home of [[Belmont Park]]. {{cite web|url=https://www.amny.com/sports/belmont-stakes-saratoga-final-time-2026/|title=Belmont Stakes to return to Saratoga for final time in 2026|website=[[AMNY]]|last=Page|first=Matthew|date=June 13, 2025|accessdate=June 20, 2025}}
The winner was [[Golden Tempo]] who was also the winner of the [[2026 Kentucky Derby]]. Winning trainer [[Cherie DeVaux]] became the second woman to train a Belmont winner, after [[Jena Antonucci]] in [[2023 Belmont Stakes | 2023]].
==Background==
New York Governor [[Kathy Hochul]] announced that the 2026 Belmont Stakes would be the third and final time Saratoga Race Course in [[Saratoga Springs, New York]], would host the race. Due to Saratoga's smaller main track, the race was run at {{convert|1+1/4|mi|furlong m|0|abbr=on}}, shorter than the usual {{convert|1+1/2|mi|furlong m|0|abbr=on}}. Belmont Park is scheduled to reopen in the fall of 2026.
The most recent time a Belmont Stakes was held elsewhere was from 1963 to 1967 at [[Aqueduct Race Track]] in [[Ozone Park, Queens]], the last time Belmont Park was under construction. Associated Press. [https://www.espn.com/horse-racing/story/_/id/39054850/renovations-move-belmont-stakes-saratoga-2024 "Renovations will move Belmont Stakes to Saratoga in 2024"]. [[ESPN]]. December 6, 2023. Retrieved on December 6, 2023.
TV broadcast was won by [[Fox Sports]], which was also show on [[Fox_Sports#United_States|Fox Sports 1]] and FS2. Streaming coverage was free on [[Fubo]]. FS2 early coverage began at 10:30 a.m. [[Eastern time zone|ET]] and the main channel Fox coverage began at 3 p.m. [[Eastern time zone|ET]].
The race itself was set to start at 7:04 p.m., also known as 'post time', but started at 7:01 p.m. due to impending weather.
== Entries ==
A field of 9 was drawn on June 1st. Kentucky Derby winner [[Golden Tempo]] drew post no. 9 and was given a morning line of 9-2. [[Renegade (horse)|Renegade]] was installed as the favorite with 2-1 morning line odds. {{cite web|url=https://www.timesunion.com/saratogatrack/article/2026-belmont-stakes-post-position-draw-live-odds-22281182.php|title=Renegade, Chief Wallabee, Kentucky Derby winner Golden Tempo lead 2026 Belmont Stakes field|date=June 6, 2026|accessdate=June 6, 2026|website=[[Times Union (Albany, New York)|Times Union]]}}
