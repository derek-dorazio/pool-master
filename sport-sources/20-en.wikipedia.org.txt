The '''2026 Tokyo Marathon''' was the 19th edition of the annual [[Tokyo Marathon|marathon race in Tokyo]], held on Sunday, 1 March 2026. A [[World Athletics Label Road Races|Platinum Label]] marathon, it was the first of seven [[World Marathon Majors]] {{Cite news |date=2024-11-03 |title=Sydney Marathon to join elite ranks of world marathon majors alongside London and New York City |url=https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-11-04/sydney-marathon-to-join-world-marathon-majors-series/104556282 |access-date=2024-11-04 |work=ABC News |language=en-AU}} events to be held in 2026. {{Cite web |title=Platinum Label Road Races Results |url=https://worldathletics.org/results/platinum-label-road-races |access-date=2025-11-16 |website=worldathletics.org}}

== Figures ==
[[Tadese Takele]] became only the second man ever to win two consecutive Tokyo Marathon titles, after [[Birhanu Legese]] in 2019 and 2020. Female winner [[Brigid Kosgei]] set a course record of 2:14:29, taking a minute off the previous mark from 2024. {{Cite web |title=東京マラソン2026 |url=https://www.marathon.tokyo/ |access-date=2026-05-18 |website=www.marathon.tokyo/ |language=en}} 5.6% of finishers finished in under 3 hours, the highest percentage in the history of the [[Tokyo Marathon]]. Overall, 73.9% of finishers were male and 26.1% were female. {{Cite web |last=Ercilla |first=Rosana |date=2026-04-07 |title=The Tokyo Marathon- tracking its growth by the data |url=https://meta-endurance.com/the-tokyo-marathon-tracking-its-growth-by-the-data/ |access-date=2026-05-18 |website=Meta Endurance |language=en-GB}}
