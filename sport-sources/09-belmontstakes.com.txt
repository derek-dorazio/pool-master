Frequently Asked Questions
How much does admission cost?
Backyard general admission tickets for Belmont Stakes Day will be available for $75. Infield general admission tickets for Belmont Stakes Day will be available for $50. Backyard general admission is free for children 12 and under on Thursday, June 3 and Friday, June 4.
Families can take advantage of the Family Four-Pack in the Belmont Park Kids Zone, which includes admission for two adults and two children. Available each day of the festival, packages will be priced at $100 June 3-4, and $200 on Belmont Stakes Day, June 5.
Where is the 2027 Belmont Stakes?
The 2027 Belmont Stakes returns home to a brand-new Belmont Park on Saturday, June 5, 2027.
Where do I buy Belmont Stakes tickets?
Tickets are available at BelmontStakes.com/Tickets via Ticketmaster . By selecting your preferred ticket type via this website, you are securing official tickets to the 2027 Belmont Stakes at Belmont Park.
Verified resale tickets may be available through Ticketmaster. Resale may reflect prices above face value.
Ticket Cancellation Policy
The date and time of any stakes race or other track event is subject to change based on, among other things, force majeure events (including, without limitation, acts of God and other unforeseen circumstances or causes beyond reasonable control). No refunds will be permitted based on such scheduling changes.
In the event that racing is cancelled at any time prior to park opening, or is taking place at a NYRA racetrack without permitted patronage to the general public, and NYRA does not intend to reschedule such racing events, NYRA will issue ticket credits or refunds solely for all tickets/pre-paid parking purchased directly through the NYRA Box Office, or a NYRA Sales Representative, for the specific event days that are affected by such situation. In such cases, tickets purchased via third-party vendors are not eligible for a refund from NYRA and customers must directly contact the third-party vendors for assistance.
