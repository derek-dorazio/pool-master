[[Elena Rybakina]] defeated two-time defending champion [[Aryna Sabalenka]] {{cite news |last=Jurejko |first=Jonathan |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/articles/c4gzpd3ln3wo |title=Sabalenka keeps cool to retain US Open title|work=BBC Sport|date=6 September 2025 |accessdate=6 September 2025}} in the final, 6–4, 5–7, 6–2 to win the women's singles tennis title at the [[2026 US Open (tennis)|2026 US Open]]. It was her third [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title (and second of the season, having also won the [[2026 Australian Open – Women's singles|Australian Open]]) and 14th [[WTA Tour]] title overall. {{Cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4575707/rybakina-halts-sabalenkas-three-peat-bid-to-capture-first-us-open-title|title=The new No. 1 has a new US Open trophy: Rybakina ends Sabalenka's reign|website=WTA Tennis|accessdate=12 September 2026}} {{Cite web|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2026-09-12/rybakina_defeats_two-time_defending_champion_sabalenka_to_win_2026_us_open.html|title=Rybakina defeats two-time defending champion Sabalenka to win 2026 US Open|website=usopen.org|accessdate=12 September 2026}} Rybakina was the sixth women's singles player (since 1988) to win both [[Hardcourt|hardcourt]] majors in the same year, with Sabalenka and Rybakina also being the first pair of women's singles players to contest both hardcourt major finals in the same year since [[Steffi Graf]] and [[Arantxa Sánchez Vicario]] in [[1994 WTA Tour|1994]]. This was also the first [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] final contested by the top two seeds since [[2013 US Open – Women's singles|2013]]. {{Cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4575242/us-open-final-preview-aryna-sabalenka-vs-elena-rybakina|title=Making the case for Sabalenka and Rybakina in the US Open final|website=WTA Tennis|accessdate=11 September 2026}} {{Cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4574905/us-open-final-preview-2026-aryna-sabalenka-elena-rybakina-start-time-prize-money-and-what-to-know|title=Aryna Sabalenka vs. Elena Rybakina: Everything to know about the US Open final|website=WTA Tennis|accessdate=11 September 2026}}
Rybakina attained the [[List of WTA number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1]] singles ranking for the first time by reaching the semifinals, becoming the 30th player to attain the top ranking and the first Kazakhstani player to achieve a world No. 1 ranking in any discipline. {{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4574266/the-long-methodical-road-that-carried-elena-rybakina-to-no-1|title=The long, methodical road that carried Elena Rybakina to No. 1|date=9 September 2026|website=WTA Tennis}} Sabalenka, [[Jessica Pegula]], and [[Coco Gauff]] were also in contention for the top ranking at the beginning of the tournament.
Aged 46, [[Venus Williams]] was the oldest woman to compete in the US Open singles main draw since [[Renée Richards]] in [[1981 US Open – Women's singles|1981]], after receiving a wildcard into the main draw. {{cite web|url= https://www.foxsports.com/articles/tennis/venus-williams-gets-a-us-open-singles-wild-card-at-46-with-maybe-a-spot-being-held-for-serena|title=Venus Williams gets a US Open singles wild card at 46, with maybe a spot being held for Serena|work=foxsports.com|date=18 August 2026}} Starting at 12:13 AM, the first-round match between Williams and [[Sofia Kenin]] set the record for the latest start in tournament history, surpassing the previous record (12:08 AM) set by the third-round match between Sabalenka and [[Ekaterina Alexandrova]] in [[2024 US Open – Women's singles|2024]]. {{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4569441/kenin-denies-williams-comeback-to-reach-us-open-second-round|title=Kenin holds off Venus Williams in record-late US Open start|date=31 August 2026|website=WTA Tennis}} [[Zheng Qinwen]] was the first qualifier to reach the quarterfinals since [[Emma Raducanu]] in [[2021 US Open – Women's singles|2021]]. {{Cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4573441/zheng-rallies-from-5-0-again-beats-swiatek-in-straight-sets-to-reach-us-open-quarters|title=Zheng rallies from 5-0 again, beats Swiatek in straight sets to reach US Open quarters|publisher=WTA Tennis|date=7 September 2026}}
All of the top four seeds reached the semifinals for the first time at the US Open since [[1975 US Open – Women's singles|1975]] and at any major since the [[2009 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|2009 Wimbledon Championships]]. {{Cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4573859/us-open-semifinals-sabalenka-pegula-gauff-rybakina-making-the-case-for-the-final-four|title=US Open semifinals: Who of the top four will play in the season's last Grand Slam final?|publisher=WTA Tennis|date=7 September 2026}}
It was also the first time at the US Open since [[1998 US Open – Women's singles|1998]] that the top six seeds reached the quarterfinals. {{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/articles/crk368k4e42o#google_vignette |title=Gauff's statement win completes stacked US Open last eight|website=[[BBC Sport]]|date=7 September 2026}}
26 out of the 32 seeds reached the third round, the joint-highest number of seeds at a major since the introduction of the 32-seed system in [[2001 Wimbledon Championships – Women's singles|2001]], {{Cite Instagram|user=womtennis|url=https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc3cUu8lF6P/|postid= Dc3cUu8lF6P|title=Stellar performance from the seeds this year, with 26/32 seeded players making it to the third round, the joint-highest number of seeded players… |date=6 September 2026|accessdate=7 September 2026}} equaling the [[2007 French Open – Women's singles|2007 French Open]]. {{Cite web | last1 = Gehl | first1 = Clément | title = US Open: 26 Seeds Reach the Third Round, a Feat Unseen Since 2007 | publisher = TennisTemple | date = 2026-09-04 | url = https://en.tennistemple.com/actu/us-open-26-seeds-reach-the-third-round-a-feat/XtL0 | accessdate = 2026-09-08}}
