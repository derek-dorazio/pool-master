Marine Fauthoux (born 23 January 2001) is a French basketball player for Galatasaray of the Women's Basketball Super League and the New York Liberty in WNBA . She has played for the French national team in the Tokyo 2020 and the Paris 2024 Olympic games. [ 1 ]
She is the daughter of Frédéric Fauthoux , former point guard of Élan Béarnais and of the French national team , now head coach of JL Bourg Basket and France men's national basketball team.
Career
[ edit ]
On July 28, 2026, she signed a contract with the Turkish giant Galatasaray . [ 2 ] Before joining the Turkish team, she made her debut in the 2026 WNBA season with the New York Liberty, the same team that drafted her in 2021, imitating her teammate Marine Johannès .
International
[ edit ]
She represented France at the FIBA Women's EuroBasket 2019 . [ 3 ] [ 4 ]
