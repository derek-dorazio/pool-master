The '''2026 NCAA Division I Women's Tennis Championships''' were the women's [[NCAA Division I Women's Tennis Championship|tennis tournaments]], the women's singles and doubles tournament was played in the fall from November 18 to November 23, 2025, in [[Orlando, Florida]] at the [[USTA National Campus]]. {{Cite web|url=https://theacc.com/news/2025/11/13/2025-ncaa-division-i-womens-tennis-individual-championships-to-begin-november-18.aspx|title=2025 NCAA Women’s Tennis Individual Championships to Begin November 18|publisher=ACC|date=November 17, 2025 |access-date=May 12, 2026}} The women's team tournament was played in the spring from May 1 to May 17, 2026, at campus sites and [[Athens, Georgia]] at the Dan Magill Tennis Complex hosted by [[University of Georgia]]. {{Cite web|url=https://www.ncaa.com/news/tennis-women/article/2026-05-09/2026-ncaa-di-womens-tennis-championship-bracket-schedule-scores|title=2026 NCAA DI women's tennis championship: Bracket, schedule, scores|publisher=NCAA|date=May 9, 2026 |access-date=May 12, 2026}} It was the 44th edition of the [[NCAA Division I women's tennis championships|NCAA Division I Women's Tennis Championship]]. As part of a two-year pilot program, the women's singles and doubles championships were conducted in the fall of 2025, while the team championships were played in the spring of 2026. {{cite web|url=https://www.ncaa.com/news/tennis-women/article/2025-11-10/2025-ncaa-division-i-womens-tennis-singles-and-doubles-championships-selections |title=2025 NCAA Division I Women’s Tennis Singles and Doubles Championships Selections|website=NCAA.com |accessdate=May 13, 2026}}
== Women's team championship ==
There were 64 teams selected for the women's team championship, 30 of which were automatic qualifiers from each Division 1 conference. The remaining 34 teams were selected at-large. Teams played two rounds of single-elimination matches in groups of four on May 1–2 or May 2–3 at campus sites; the winners of those regionals advanced to a super-regional round on May 9 or 10, also held at campus sites. The remaining eight teams advanced to the championship rounds in [[Athens, Georgia]] on May 15–17. {{Cite web|url=https://www.ncaa.com/news/tennis-women/article/2026-04-27/teams-announced-2026-ncaa-division-i-womens-tennis-championship |title=Teams announced for 2026 NCAA Division I women’s tennis championship|website=NCAA.com|date=April 27, 2026|access-date=May 12, 2026}}

==Women's singles championship==
There were 64 singles players that qualified for the women's singles championship, via competition in approved [[Intercollegiate Tennis Association|ITA]] qualifying pathway events. The tournament was played in the Fall of 2025 from November 18 to 23 in [[Orlando, Florida]].
[[North Carolina Tar Heels women's tennis|North Carolina]] senior [[Reese Brantmeier]] won the women's singles title against [[California Golden Bears women's tennis|California]] senior Berta Passola Folch 6–3, 6–3. {{cite web|url=https://theacc.com/news/2025/11/23/north-carolinas-brantmeier-wins-2025-ncaa-womens-tennis-singles-championship-title.aspx |title=North Carolina's Brantmeier wins 2025 NCAA Women's Tennis Singles Championship Title |website=theACC.com |accessdate=May 13, 2026}}

==Women's doubles championship==
32 doubles teams qualified for the women's doubles championship, via competition in approved [[Intercollegiate Tennis Association|ITA]] qualifying pathway events. The tournament was played was played at the same time as the singles championship in the Fall of 2025 from November 18 to 23 in [[Orlando, Florida]].
Gabbi Broadfoot and Tori Osuigwe of [[NC State Wolfpack women's tennis|NC State]] beat Celia-Belle Mohr and Sophia Webster of [[Vanderbilt Commodores women's tennis|Vanderbilt]], to win the women's double title. {{cite web|title=National Champions! Broadfoot and Osuigwe Secure Second NCAA Title in Program History|url=https://gopack.com/news/2025/11/23/womens-tennis-national-champions-broadfoot-and-osuigwe-secure-second-ncaa-title-in-program-history?cs=1&hl=en-US&biw=680&bih=622|website=gopack.com|publisher=NC State Wolfpack Athletics|date=November 23, 2025|access-date=May 13, 2026}}
