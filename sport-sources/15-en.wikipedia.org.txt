The '''2026 Berlin Marathon''' was the 52nd edition of the annual [[Berlin Marathon|marathon race in Berlin]], held on Sunday, September 27, 2026. A [[World Athletics Label Road Races|Platinum Label]] marathon, it was one of the six [[World Marathon Majors]] events scheduled for 2026.
Ethiopian runner [[Tigst Assefa]] won the women's race running the 3rd all-time best marathon time and a course record of 2:11:04 despite acquiring an injury which forced her to adopt a visible limp in the final two kilometers. {{Cite web |date=2026-09-27 |title=Injured Assefa narrowly misses world record in Berlin |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/athletics/articles/c6rr4y59n0r0o |access-date=2026-09-27 |website=[[BBC Sport]]}} In the men's race, the 2021 Berlin marathon champion [[Guye Adola]] ran a personal best to capture his second title in the event. {{Cite web |date=2026-09-27 |title=Berlin Marathon 2026: Ethiopia shines as Tigst Assefa narrowly misses world record and Guye Adola claims second title |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/news/berlin-marathon-2026-ethiopia-shines-as-tigst-assefa-narrowly-misses-world-record-and-guye-adola-claims-second-title |access-date=2026-09-27 |website=[[Olympics]]}} Swiss wheelchair athletes [[Marcel Hug]] and [[Catherine Debrunner]] won their respective races.
== Competitors ==
Defending champion [[Sabastian Sawe]] was forced to withdraw from the event due to injury a month out from the race. {{Cite web |date=2026-08-27 |title=Sabastian Sawe withdraws from the BMW BERLIN-MARATHON |url=https://www.bmw-berlin-marathon.com/en/news-media/news/detail/sabastian-sawe-withdraws-from-the-bmw-berlin-marathon |access-date=2026-08-27 |website=[[Berlin Marathon]]}} A few days prior, former champion [[Kenenisa Bekele]] was also forced to withdraw due to an Achilles injury. {{Cite web |date=2026-08-24 |title=Kenenisa Bekele withdraws from Berlin Marathon |url=https://runningmagazine.ca/the-scene/kenenisa-bekele-withdraws-from-berlin-marathon/ |access-date=2026-08-27 |website=Canadian Running Magazine}} In the women's race, [[Esther Pfeiffer]] was aiming to break the German women's national record set by [[Irina Mikitenko]] in 2008 but was forced to withdraw mid-race due to physical conditions. {{Cite web |date=2026-09-27 |title=Outstanding Performances at the BMW BERLIN-MARATHON 2026 |url=https://www.bmw-berlin-marathon.com/en/news-media/news/detail/outstanding-performances-at-the-bmw-berlin-marathon-2026 |access-date=2026-09-27 |website=[[Berlin Marathon]]}}
