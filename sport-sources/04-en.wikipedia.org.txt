The '''2026 US Open – Women's singles qualifying''' is a series of tennis matches that took place from 24 to 28 August 2026 (was originally to end on 27 August, but got rescheduled due to heavy rain) to determine the 16 qualifiers into the main draw of the [[2026 US Open – Women's singles|women's singles tournament]]. {{cite web |title=2026 tournament schedule |url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/about/eventschedule.html |access-date=2026-07-16 |website=usopen.org}}
16 out of the 128 qualifiers who competed in this knock-out tournament secured a [[2026 US Open – Women's singles|main draw place]]. {{cite web|url=https://mytennishq.com/how-do-tennis-players-qualify-for-grand-slams/|title=How Do Tennis Players Qualify For Grand Slams?|first=Gui|last=Hadlich|access-date=2023-05-19|website=My Tennis HQ}} {{cite web|url=https://thestadiumreviews.com/blogs/info/what-is-a-qualifier-in-tennis/|title=What is a Qualifier in Tennis?|date=2022-06-02|access-date=2023-05-19|website=The Stadium Reviews}} {{cite web|url=https://tennispredict.com/how-tennis-players-qualifies-for-grand-slams/|title=How Tennis Players Qualify For Grand Slams|first=Fred|last=Simonsson|date=2023-04-24|access-date=2023-05-19|website=TennisPredict.com}} {{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/lucky-loser-explained-2023|title=Lucky Loser: Explaining The Greatest Term In Tennis|date=2023-05-06|access-date=2023-05-19|website=ATP Tour}}
This marks the first time [[Tennis at the 2024 Summer Olympics|2024 Olympic gold medalist]] [[Zheng Qinwen]] contested a major qualifying competition since the [[2022 Australian Open – Women's singles qualifying|2022 Australian Open]].

==Qualifying wild card challenge==
The US Open qualifying wild card challenge was a new competition featuring rising American men and women competing for a wild card in the US Open Qualifying Tournament.
The challenge featured four men and four women competing in six total matches — men’s and women’s semifinals and finals — with the winners receiving a wild card into the US Open qualifying event.
