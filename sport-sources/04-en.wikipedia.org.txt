The 2027 NASCAR Cup Series will be the 79th season for NASCAR professional stock car racing in the United States and the 56th season for the modern-era Cup Series. The preseason will feature the Wheatley Vodka Clash at Daytona International Speedway , the first time back for the event since 2021 , on February 13. It will be followed by the season-opening 69th running of the Daytona 500 on February 21, and will end at Homestead–Miami Speedway on November 14. [ 1 ]
The season will mark the first full time season without Kyle Busch starting a race since the 2002 season, following his death on May 21, 2026. [ 2 ] [ 3 ] [ 4 ]

Changes
[ edit ]
Team changes
[ edit ]
On January 16, 2026, Ford announced their Mustang Dark Horse SC will be debuted this season for all of its teams. [ 46 ]
On September 29, 2026, Brad Keselowski announced that he will leave the RFK Racing ownership group. [ 24 ]
Driver changes
[ edit ]
Following the death of Kyle Busch on May 21, 2026, Richard Childress Racing 's No. 8, now renumbered to the No. 33, was left without a driver. [ 47 ] Austin Hill would take his place for that weekend's Coca-Cola 600 , as was announced just hours before his passing, and later the remainder of the season. [ 48 ] Hill was announced to pilot the car in 2027 on August 24. [ 16 ]
Riley Herbst would not return to 23XI Racing for 2027, with Corey Heim stepping up to fill his slot at the team. [ 49 ] On September 3, 2026, it was confirmed Herbst would be driving for Legacy Motor Club in their newly opened third team.
Josh Berry would not return to Wood Brothers Racing for 2027, with Jesse Love stepping up to fill his slot at the team. Berry would move to LMC replacing John Hunter Nemechek . [ 35 ] [ 36 ]
Brad Keselowski announced on September 29, 2026, that he will be leaving RFK Racing as driver and owner in 2026. [ 24 ]
Rookies
[ edit ]
See also: NASCAR Rookie of the Year § Cup Series
Jesse Love is the only expected rookie for 2027 so far, having signed a deal to race with Wood Brothers Racing in place of the outgoing Josh Berry . Austin Hill and Corey Heim will both be racing in their first full time Cup Series season in 2027, but will not be eligible for Rookie of the Year due to them both having already surpassed the 12 race limit.

Notes
[ edit ]
Schedule changes
[ edit ]
The races at Richmond Raceway , Watkins Glen International and New Hampshire Motor Speedway return to " The Chase " for the first time since 2021 , 2024 and 2025 respectively. [ 53 ] [ 54 ] [ 55 ] The Wheatley Vodka Clash will return to Daytona International Speedway for the first time since 2020 , replacing Bowman Gray Stadium . [ 56 ] [ 51 ] Bristol Motor Speedway 's Bass Pro Shops Night Race returns to its traditional August weekend, after six years in The Chase. [ 57 ] The NASCAR All-Star Race returns to North Wilkesboro Speedway as it was from 2023 to 2025 . [ 58 ] Due to that change, Dover Motor Speedway regained its points race after a one year-hiatus.
