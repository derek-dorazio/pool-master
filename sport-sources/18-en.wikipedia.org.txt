The '''2025–26 PWHL season''' was the third season of operation of the [[Professional Women's Hockey League]]. The league expanded to eight teams with the addition of the [[Seattle Torrent]] and the [[Vancouver Goldeneyes]]. The regular season began on November 21, 2025. The [[2026 Walter Cup playoffs|Walter Cup playoffs]] began on April 30 and ended on May 20, 2026, with the [[Montreal Victoire]] winning their first [[Walter Cup]].
== League business ==
* On April 15, 2025, the PWHL announced that the [[2025 PWHL Draft]] would be held June 24 at the [[Rideau Carleton Raceway|Hard Rock Hotel & Casino]] in [[Ottawa]]. {{cite web |url=https://www.thepwhl.com/en/news/2025/april/15/2025-pwhl-draft-to-be-held-june-24-in-ottawa |title=2025 PWHL Draft To Be Held June 24 In Ottawa |website=[[Professional Women's Hockey League]] |date=April 15, 2025 |access-date=June 1, 2025 |quote=Hard Rock Hotel & Casino will be Official Host}}
* On April 23, 2025, the PWHL announced that their seventh team and first expansion franchise would be located in [[Vancouver]], and begin play in the 2025–26 season. The [[Vancouver Goldeneyes]] would play their home games out of the [[Pacific Coliseum]], which was the home arena of the NHL's [[Vancouver Canucks]] from 1970 to 1995. {{cite news |url=https://www.cbc.ca/sports/hockey/pwhl/pwhl-expansion-vancouver-1.7516319 |title=Vancouver announced as newest PWHL franchise for 2025-26 season |work=[[CBC Sports]] |last=Donkin |first=Karissa |date=April 23, 2025 |access-date=July 23, 2025}}
* On April 30, 2025, the PWHL announced that their eighth team and second expansion franchise would be located in [[Seattle]], and begin play in the 2025–26 season. The [[Seattle Torrent]] would play their home games out of [[Climate Pledge Arena]], which is the home arena of the NHL's [[Seattle Kraken]] and the WNBA's [[Seattle Storm]]. {{cite news |url=https://www.cbc.ca/sports/hockey/pwhl/pwhl-seattle-announcement-2025-1.7522028 |title=PWHL continues West Coast expansion, adding franchise in Seattle |work=CBC Sports |last=Donkin |first=Karissa |date=April 30, 2025 |access-date=July 23, 2025}}

== Playoffs ==
{{main|2026 Walter Cup playoffs}}
=== Bracket ===
In each round, teams compete in a best-of-five series following a 2–2–1 format. The team with [[home advantage|home ice advantage]] plays at home for games one and two (and game five, if necessary), and the other team is at home for games three and four. The top four teams make the playoffs.
In the first round, by virtue of finishing first in the regular season standings, the [[Montreal Victoire]] have earned the right to chose their first round opponent.

==Broadcast rights==
Source: {{cite web |title=PWHL Announces 2025–26 Broadcast Schedule |url=https://www.thepwhl.com/en/news/2025/november/18/pwhl-announces-2025-26-broadcast-schedule |date=November 18, 2025 |access-date=November 18, 2025 |website=ThePWHL.com}}
===Canada===
====English====
For the 2025–26 season, games in Canada were broadcast in English across [[The Sports Network|TSN]], [[Sportsnet]], [[Sports on Prime Video|Prime Video]], and [[CBC Television|CBC]]. TSN broadcast 54 regular season games, along with one PWHL Playoff semifinal and all games from the PWHL Finals. Prime Video broadcast 19 games, along with the other PWHL Playoff semifinal. Sportsnet broadcast 30 regular season games, while CBC or [[CBC Gem]] broadcast 17 games.
====French====
In Canada, all games featuring the Montréal Victoire will also be broadcast in French. These games are split between [[Réseau des sports|RDS]], [[Ici Radio-Canada Télé|Ici Télé]] and Prime Video. RDS broadcast 17 games, along with one PWHL Playoff semifinal and all games from the PWHL Finals. Prime Video broadcast six games, along with the other PWHL Playoff semifinal. ICI TÉLÉ or ICI TOU.TV broadcast seven games.
===United States===
====Local====
Locally, [[Boston Fleet]] games were broadcast on [[NESN]], with select games simulcast on [[WSBK-TV]]. [[Minnesota Frost]] games were broadcast on [[FanDuel Sports Network North]], with select games simulcast on [[KMSP-TV]]. [[New York Sirens]] games were broadcast on [[MSG Network]], with select games simulcast on [[WWOR-TV]]. Seattle Torrent games are primarily broadcast on [[KZJO]], with six games airing instead on [[KONG (TV)|KONG]].
====National====
Nationally, all games in the United States were broadcast on [[YouTube]]. In addition, some games were shown out-of-market on select [[FanDuel Sports Network]] stations, [[SportsNet Pittsburgh]], select [[Gray Broadcast Sports Networks]] stations, and select [[Scripps Sports]] stations.
In March 2026, the PWHL reached a test agreement with U.S. [[broadcast network|network]] [[Ion Television]], which already owned the rights to several leading women's sports properties. This represented the circuit's first true national TV exposure in the country. It entailed the broadcasting of a regular season game {{cite web | url =https://www.reuters.com/sports/sirens-victoire-play-pwhls-first-game-televised-nationally-us--flm-2026-03-12/ |title =Sirens, Victoire to play in PWHL's first game televised nationally in US |author= |date =March 12, 2026 |website =reuters.com |access-date =July 1, 2025}} {{cite web | url =https://nypost.com/2026/03/28/sports/ions-push-into-pwhl-another-big-win-for-womens-sports-coverage/ |title =ION's push into PWHL another big win for women’s sports coverage |last1 =Lehman |first1 =Jonathan |date =March 28, 2026 |work =[[New York Post]] |access-date =July 1, 2025}} and the entire Walter Cup Finals. {{cite web | url =https://usatoday.com/story/sports/hockey/2026/03/18/pwhl-finals-national-tv-where-to-watch/89207992007/ |title =PWHL's Walter Cup Finals will air on national TV, a first for league |last1 =Armour |first1 =Nancy |date =March 18, 2026 |work =USA Today |access-date =July 1, 2025}} {{cite web | url =https://www.theixsports.com/the-ice-garden/pwhl/pwhl-walter-cup-finals-to-air-on-ion-in-usa/ |title =PWHL Walter Cup Finals to air on ION in USA |last1 =Burgess |first1 =Melissa |date =March 18, 2026 |website =theicegarden.com |via=theixsports.com |access-date =July 1, 2025}} The league's U.S. national TV debut, a March 28 game between Montreal and New York, drew 133,000 viewers. {{cite web | url =https://www.advanced-television.com/2026/06/04/nielsen-viewership-growth-across-nba-nhl-indycar-golf-in-us/ |title =Nielsen: Viewership growth across NBA, NHL, IndyCar, golf in US |author= |date =June 4, 2026 |website =advanced-television.com |access-date =July 1, 2025}}
===International===
In Czechia and Slovakia, games were broadcast on [[Nova Sport (Czech Republic and Slovakia)|Nova Sport]]. Outside of Canada, Czechia, and Slovakia, games were broadcast on the PWHL [[YouTube]] channel and thepwhl.com.
