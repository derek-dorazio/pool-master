The '''2026 Chicago Marathon''' is the 48th edition of the annual [[Chicago Marathon]]. The race is the sixth of seven [[World Marathon Majors]] held in 2026 and is a [[World Athletics Label Road Races|platinum-label]] race. {{Cite web |title=Platinum Label Road Races {{!}} Results |url=https://worldathletics.org/results/platinum-label-road-races |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=worldathletics.org}}
An estimated 55,000 people ran in Chicago for the 2026 annual marathon in temperatures which were slightly warmer than ideal. {{Cite web |title=2026 Bank of America Chicago Marathon draws 55,000 runners to city streets |url=https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/2026-bank-of-america-chicago-marathon-steps-off/|access-date=2026-10-11 |website=[[CBS News]]}} [[Amos Kipruto]] won his first Chicago marathon title in the men's race while [[Hawi Feysa]] repeated her victory from the previous year. American runner [[Emma Grace Hurley]] ran the fastest debut marathon in U.S. women's history. {{Cite web |title=Chicago Marathon: Amos Kipruto, Hawi Feysa win; Emma Grace Hurley runs fastest debut in U.S. history |url=https://www.nbcsports.com/olympics/news/chicago-marathon-2026-amos-kipruto-hawi-feysa|access-date=2026-10-11 |website=[[NBC Sports]]}} Both champions ran personal bests. In the wheelchair races, [[Marcel Hug]] captured his 42nd major victory and [[Catherine Debrunner]] her 14th major victory. {{Cite web |title=2026 Chicago Marathon Results |url=https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/2026-chicago-marathon-results-141804313.html|access-date=2026-10-11 |website=[[Yahoo News]]}}
==Background==
Thousands of hotel workers went on strike the Friday morning prior to the event demanding new contracts in anticipation of the unusually busy weekend. {{Cite web |title=Hotel workers on strike ahead of Chicago Marathon weekend |url=https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/hotel-workers-strike-chicago-marathon-weekend/|access-date=2026-10-11 |website=[[CBS News]]}} European record holder [[Bashir Abdi]] and last year's bronze medalist [[Alex Masai]] were forced to withdraw from the race ahead of race day. {{Cite web |title=Bashir Abdi Withdraws From the Chicago Marathon Six Days Before the Race |url=https://marathonhandbook.com/bashir-abdi-withdraws-chicago-marathon/ |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=marathonhandbook.com}} American Olympian [[Clayton Young]] decided to race despite a hamstring injury. {{Cite web |title=Chicago Marathon 2026: Clayton Young still set on chasing American marathon record despite hamstring setback |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/news/chicago-marathon-2026-clayton-young-american-marathon-record-attempt |access-date=2026-10-11 |website=[[Olympics]]}}
