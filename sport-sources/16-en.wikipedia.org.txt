The '''2026 Preakness Stakes''' was the 151st running of the [[Preakness Stakes]], a Grade I stakes race for three-year-old [[Thoroughbred]]s at a distance of {{frac|1|3|16}} miles ({{frac|9|1|2}} furlongs; 1,911 meters). It took place on May 16, 2026. For the first time in the race's history, it took place at [[Laurel Park (race track)|Laurel Park]] due to construction at the race's usual home of [[Pimlico Race Course]]. Sears, Bryan P. [https://marylandmatters.org/2025/05/06/state-officials-moving-forward-with-plans-to-modernize-pimlico/ "State officials moving forward with plans to modernize Pimlico"]. ''Maryland Matters''. May 6, 2025. Retrieved on June 25, 2025. O'Neil, Dana. [https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/6359231/2025/05/16/preakness-pimlico-triple-crown/ "After 150th Preakness, a wrecking ball and ‘philosophical dilemma’ for Triple Crown’s middle child"]. ''The Athletic''. May 16, 2025. Retrieved on June 25, 2025.
It was announced on May 6, 2026, that [[Golden Tempo]], the [[2026 Kentucky Derby]] winner, would not run in the Preakness, thus giving up the chance at the [[Triple Crown of Thoroughbred Racing (United States)|Triple Crown]]. {{Cite tweet |user= reredevaux |number=2052099191153860819 |title= Preakness announcement|author=[[Cherie DeVaux]]|date=2026-05-06|access-date=2026-05-30}} [[Napoleon Solo (horse)|Napoleon Solo]] won the race. {{Cite web |last=Ng |first=Greg |date=2026-05-16 |title=Preakness winner emerges from the first full field in five years |url=https://www.wbaltv.com/article/who-won-the-preakness-2026-napoleon-solo-laurel-park/71319179 |access-date=2026-05-16 |website=WBAL |language=en}}
== Field ==
The field for the race was drawn on May 11, 2026. A full field of 14 was entered, with Iron Honor installed as the 9–2 morning line favorite drawing post no. 9. {{cite web|url=https://www.courier-journal.com/story/sports/horses/horse-racing/2026/05/11/preakness-stakes-2026-odds-post-positions-field-laurel-park-kentucky-derby-horses-ocelli-robusta/89919962007/|title=Preakness 2026 odds, favorite, post positions for field at Laurel Park|first=Jason|last=Frakes|date=May 11, 2026|accessdate=May 11, 2026|website=[[Courier Journal]]}}
