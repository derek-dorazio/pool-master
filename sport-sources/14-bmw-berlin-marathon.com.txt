To the GENERALI BERLIN-MARATHON 2027
Thank you BMW - welcome Generali!
We are very excited to look to the future together with Generali. At the same time, we would like to say a heartfelt thank you to our long-standing partner BMW – for the trust they have placed in us and for a collaboration shaped by shared successes and unforgettable moments.
On September 26, 2027, a new chapter begins with the GENERALI BERLIN-MARATHON – bringing new stories and the unique energy that is created when people from all over the world come together to run a marathon through Berlin.
As part of the Abbott World Marathon Majors, the GENERALI BERLIN-MARATHON is one of the world’s most prestigious marathons. Here, extraordinary performances meet personal success stories, supported by millions of spectators along the course.
The registration for the lottery 2027 is now open.
