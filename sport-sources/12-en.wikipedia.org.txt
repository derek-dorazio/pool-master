==Race background==
{{main|Indianapolis 500|Indianapolis Motor Speedway}}
[[File:2018Indy500Start.jpg|thumb|Turn one at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway (''pictured in 2018'').]]
The [[Indianapolis 500]], commonly called the Indy 500, is held at the [[Indianapolis Motor Speedway]], a {{convert|2.5|mi|2|adj=mid}} paved [[Oval track racing|oval]]. First held in [[1911 Indianapolis 500|1911]], it is currently a points-paying race of the [[2025 IndyCar Series|NTT IndyCar Series]]. The event is contested by [[American open-wheel car racing|"Indy cars"]], a [[Formula racing|formula]] of professional-level, single-seat, open [[cockpit]], [[Open-wheel car|open-wheel]], purpose-built race cars. The race is the most prestigious event of the [[IndyCar Series|IndyCar]] calendar, and one of the oldest and [[Triple Crown of Motorsport|most important]] automobile races in the world. {{cite web |last= |date=August 3, 2018 |title=USA Today Readers Name Indianapolis 500 As World's Best Motorsports Race In Poll |url=https://www.indianapolismotorspeedway.com/news-multimedia/news/2018/08/03/usa-today-readers-name-indy-500-worlds-best-motorsports-race-2018 |url-status=live |website=Indianapolis Motor Speedway |access-date=August 19, 2025 |archive-date=May 12, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210512120042/https://www.indianapolismotorspeedway.com/news-multimedia/news/2018/08/03/usa-today-readers-name-indy-500-worlds-best-motorsports-race-2018 }} The race [[Indianapolis 500 traditions|traditionally]] has a field of 33 cars. {{Cite web |last=Martin |first=Bruce |date=6 May 2022 |title=How The Indianapolis 500 Got Its 33rd Entrant |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/brucemartin/2022/05/06/how-the-indianapolis-500-got-its-33rd-entrant/ |access-date=27 May 2026 |website=[[Forbes]]}}
===Track improvements===
According to Indianapolis Motor Speedway president [[Doug Boles]], track maintenance crews have been investigating bumps in Turn 2 since [[2019 Indianapolis 500|2019]]. During track evaluations prior to the [[2025 Indianapolis 500|2025 race]], the Turn 2 bump in question was considerably higher than in the past. The pavement was pressed in an attempt to reduce the severity. In July 2025, during the [[2025 Brickyard 400|Brickyard 400]], the severity of the bumps in Turn 2 were reported by drivers and crew chiefs during the track walk and all sessions. {{cite web|url=https://fox59.com/news/indy-500-sports/historic-bricks-unearthed-at-the-indianapolis-motor-speedway/|title=Historic bricks unearthed at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway|website=WXIN|publisher=NewsNation|date=September 23, 2025|access-date=October 20, 2025|last=Adams|first=Matt|archive-date=October 30, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251030193808/https://fox59.com/news/indy-500-sports/historic-bricks-unearthed-at-the-indianapolis-motor-speedway/|url-status=live}}
On September 23, 2025, construction crews excavated the section of track in question. It was determined that bricks originally installed in 1909 (that had been paved over by asphalt in 1937) had expanded and split due to moisture, which raised the pavement. The damaged bricks were removed and a new base was applied. The section of the track was repaved, followed by diamond grinding to blend the new section with the adjacent asphalt. The track was allowed to cure for four weeks before the late October Speedway tests for Firestone. {{cite web |last1=Smith |first1=Eric |title=IMS testing Track News Test Patterns: Smooth Sailing at IMS with Track Patch, Tires, More |url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2025/10/10-21-ims-oval-testpatterns |website=IndyCar |publisher=Penske Entertainment |access-date=2025-10-21 |ref=Oct25Tests |archive-date=October 22, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251022030607/https://www.indycar.com/news/2025/10/10-21-ims-oval-testpatterns |url-status=live }}

*A modified qualifying format was announced on May 11, due to a lack of entries to necessitate the Last Chance Qualifying session that had been featured in previous years. During Saturday qualifying, those qualifying positions 1–15 would advance to Sunday qualifying sessions, while positions 16–33 would be locked in to their positions. During Sunday qualifying, the drivers qualified positions 10–15 on Saturday would participate in a "Final 15" session, in which the fastest three cars advance to the Top 12 session later in the day, while positions 13–15 would be locked in to their spots. The remaining session would proceed as in previous years; the fastest six cars in the Top 12 session moved on to the Fast Six, while positions 7–12 were locked. The Fast Six session would then determine starting positions 1–6. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/11/indycar-tweaks-indy-500-qualifying-format|title=IndyCar tweaks Indy 500 qualifying format|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=May 11, 2026|accessdate=May 11, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
*During the final 60 minutes of practice each day, drivers were permitted to practice entering the pit area off of turn four. This simulates the method of entering the pits used on race day. At all other times, drivers were still required to enter the pits at the warm-up lane in turn three – with exceptions only for emergency service. Previously, pitting off of turn four (during practice) was only allowed during Post-Qualifying Practice (Monday) and on Carb Day.

===2026 IndyCar Series===
{{Main|2026 IndyCar Series}}
The 2026 Indianapolis 500 was the seventh race of the [[2026 IndyCar Series|2026 NTT IndyCar Series season]]. {{cite web|url=https://www.foxsports.com/motor/indycar/schedule|access-date=February 20, 2026|website=Fox Sports|title=NTT IndyCar Series Schedule|archive-date=February 11, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260211154019/https://www.foxsports.com/motor/indycar/schedule|url-status=live}} Four different drivers won the first six races of the season. [[Álex Palou]] won the season opener at [[2026 Firestone Grand Prix of St. Petersburg|St. Petersburg]]. [[Josef Newgarden]] won at [[2026 Good Ranchers 250|Phoenix]], and [[Kyle Kirkwood]] won at [[2026 Java House Grand Prix of Arlington|Arlington]]. Palou won for the second and third time this season at [[2026 Children's of Alabama Indy Grand Prix|Alabama]] and [[2026 Acura Grand Prix of Long Beach|Long Beach]]. The [[2026 Sonsio Grand Prix|Sonsio Grand Prix]] on the Indianapolis Motor Speedway road course was won by [[Christian Lundgaard]]. It was the final race before the "500". Palou held a 27-point margin over Kirkwood in the championship points standings going into the Indianapolis 500 - the fourth year in a row Palou has led the points standings going into the "500". {{Cite web |date=26 May 2026 |title=NTT IndyCar Series Page |url=https://www.racing-reference.info/indycar-series/ |access-date=26 May 2026 |website=racing-reference.info |archive-date=May 26, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260526145915/https://www.racing-reference.info/indycar-series/ |url-status=live }}

==Testing and Rookie Orientation==
[[File:Mick Schumacher 2024 WEC Fuji.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Mick Schumacher]] is one of four rookies entered. His father [[Michael Schumacher]] won the [[United States Grand Prix|U.S. Grand Prix]] at Indy five times.]]
Rookie rules for the Indianapolis 500 included the mandate that a driver pass a supervised high-speed oval test before he/she is allowed to participate in the official Rookie Orientation Program (ROP) at the Indianapolis Motor Speedway. {{cite news|title=2024 NTT IndyCar Series Official Rulebook|url=https://epaddock.indycar.com/docs/default-source/rules-regulations-and-policies/indycar-rulebook.pdf|publisher=IndyCar|access-date=March 12, 2024|work=Section 4.3.2.7|archive-date=March 12, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240312195958/https://epaddock.indycar.com/docs/default-source/rules-regulations-and-policies/indycar-rulebook.pdf|url-status=live}} Approved venues for the initial high-speed oval track test included [[Texas Motor Speedway]], [[Homestead–Miami Speedway]], and a few other select venues. {{cite news|title=2024 NTT IndyCar Series Official Rulebook|url=https://epaddock.indycar.com/docs/default-source/rules-regulations-and-policies/indycar-rulebook.pdf|publisher=IndyCar|access-date=March 12, 2024|work=Section 6.8.1|archive-date=March 12, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240312195958/https://epaddock.indycar.com/docs/default-source/rules-regulations-and-policies/indycar-rulebook.pdf|url-status=live}}
The Rookie Orientation Program at Indianapolis consisted of three phases. For phase 1, each driver was required to complete ten laps between {{convert|205|–|210|mph|km/h|abbr=on}}, while demonstrating satisfactory car control, proper racing line, and safe interaction with other cars on the circuit. The laps did not have to be consecutive. Phase 2 was fifteen laps between {{convert|210|–|215|mph|km/h|abbr=on}}, and phase 3 was fifteen laps over {{convert|215|mph|km/h|abbr=on}}. Veteran drivers that had not competed in an IndyCar oval race since the [[2025 Indianapolis 500|previous year's]] Indy 500 were required to take a refresher test. The refresher test consisted of phase 2 and phase 3 of the aforementioned rookie test. {{cite news|title=Larson to turn first IndyCar Series laps Thursday at IMS|url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2023/10/10-09-rop-hybrid-test-preview|publisher=|date=October 9, 2023|access-date=October 12, 2023|archive-date=October 14, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231014053307/https://www.indycar.com/News/2023/10/10-09-ROP-Hybrid-Test-Preview|url-status=live|work=IndyCar}}
===Testing – October 2025===
On October 20–21, 2025, the Speedway organized private testing. [[Álex Palou]] ([[Chip Ganassi Racing|Ganassi]]) and [[Pato O'Ward]] ([[Arrow McLaren]]) participated in tire tests for [[Firestone Tire and Rubber Company|Firestone]], including evaluating the newly repaved section of track to remove bumps in turn 2. A new tire compound was developed in order to address weight balance issues stemming from the hybrid system first used at the Speedway in 2025. [[Alexander Rossi]] ([[Ed Carpenter Racing|ECR]]) and [[Takuma Sato]] ([[Rahal Letterman Lanigan Racing|RLLR]]) tested [[brake]] and [[Shock absorber|damper]] components for speedway-specific braking systems (used at Indianapolis and [[IndyCar Series at Nashville Superspeedway|Nashville]]). {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2025/10/21/o-ward-happy-after-productive-indycar-tire-test-at-ims|title=O'Ward, Palou encouraged after productive IndyCar tire test at IMS|website=Racer|publisher=[[Racer (magazine)|Racer Media & Marketing, Inc.]]|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=October 21, 2025|access-date=October 21, 2025}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
===Rookie oval tests===

Two drivers participated: [[Dennis Hauger]] ([[Dale Coyne Racing|Coyne]]) and [[Caio Collet]] ([[A. J. Foyt Racing|Foyt]]). No incidents were reported. Both drivers completed their evaluations and were approved to take the Rookie Orientation Program at the April Open Test at Indianapolis. {{cite news|url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/02/02-05-schumacher-oval-test|title=Test Patterns: Scott Dixon, Alexander Rossi Never Tire of Phoenix|first=Paul|last=Kelly|work=IndyCar|date=November 7, 2025|access-date=February 20, 2026|archive-date=February 6, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260206021449/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/02/02-05-schumacher-oval-test|url-status=live}}

===Open Test Day 1 — Tuesday April 28===
[[File:Conor daly (52703152591) (cropped).jpg|thumb|right|upright|[[Conor Daly]] (''pictured in 2023'') was fastest on Day 1 of the Open Test.]]
The first day of open testing was scheduled for April 28. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2025/12/09/indycar-s-2026-testing-calendar-taking-shape|title=IndyCar's 2026 testing calendar taking shape|website=Racer|publisher=[[Racer (magazine)|Racer Media & Marketing, Inc.]]|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=December 9, 2025|access-date=January 12, 2026|archive-date=December 12, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251212173518/https://racer.com/2025/12/09/indycar-s-2026-testing-calendar-taking-shape|url-status=live}} After a five-minute period for install laps, the track was first made available for veterans from 10:05 a.m. to 12 p.m. Rookie Orientation and Refresher Tests took place from 12 p.m. to 2 p.m. The track was then open to all cars from 2 p.m. to 6 p.m. {{cite web|url=https://www.indianapolismotorspeedway.com/events/indy500/event-info/open-test|title=Open Test April 28–29|website=Indianapolis Motor Speedway|access-date=April 15, 2025|archive-date=April 19, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250419110259/https://www.indianapolismotorspeedway.com/events/indy500/event-info/open-test|url-status=live}}
*'''Weather''': {{convert|64|°F|0}}; Mostly cloudy early, fair late
*'''Summary''': The first veteran session began as scheduled at 10:05 a.m. A total of 24 drivers turned laps during the session, with [[Josef Newgarden]] running fastest at {{convert|223.415|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/28/newgarden-sets-the-pace-in-ims-open-test-morning-session|title=Newgarden sets the pace in IMS Open Test morning session|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 28, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} One minor incident occurred during the session, when [[Romain Grosjean]] collided with a bird, bringing out a caution for debris, and causing some clean up of his car to be needed. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/28/daly-leads-the-way-on-first-day-of-ims-open-test|title=Daly leads the way on first day of IMS Open Test|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 28, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
:The Rookie Orientation and Refresher Test session began immediately following the veteran's test, with nine drivers scheduled to participate. [[Jack Harvey (racing driver)|Jack Harvey]], [[Ed Carpenter (racing driver)|Ed Carpenter]], [[Ryan Hunter-Reay]], and [[Hélio Castroneves]] completed their veteran refresher tests, while [[Caio Collet]], [[Jacob Abel (racing driver)|Jacob Abel]], and [[Mick Schumacher]] completed all three phases of their rookie tests. [[Dennis Hauger]] completed phases 1 and 2 of his rookie test, and was given permission to complete the final phase during the full-field session later in the afternoon. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/28/harvey-tops-rookie-refresher-session-at-ims-open-test|title=Harvey tops Rookie/Refresher session at IMS Open Test|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 28, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} [[Katherine Legge]], the final driver required to complete a refresher test, experienced clutch problems and was unable to complete any laps at speed. As a result, she was not permitted to participate in the full-field session. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/28/technical-glitches-sideline-legge-from-ims-open-test-until-wednesday|title=Technical glitches sideline Legge from IMS Open Test until Wednesday|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 28, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
:The full-field test session began at 2:00 p.m., with the track open until 6:00 p.m. [[Conor Daly]] set the fastest speed of the day, running a lap at {{convert|225.394|mph|kph|abbr=on}} late in the day. His teammate [[Jack Harvey (racing driver)|Jack Harvey]] ran the fastest "no-tow" lap — a lap without the assistance of [[Drafting (aerodynamics)|aerodynamic drafting]] — at {{convert|220.318|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. Only one incident was reported. At 5:34 p.m., [[Christian Rasmussen (racing driver)|Christian Rasmussen]] came to a stop on track at the exit of turn 2 with a loose right-rear wheel. The car was undamaged, but needed to be towed off of the race track. {{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/conor-daly-paces-day-1-of-indy-500-open-test/10816252/|title=Conor Daly paces Day 1 of Indy 500 Open Test|first=Joey|last=Barnes|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 28, 2026|website=Motorsport}}

===Open Test Day 2 — Wednesday April 29===
[[File:Caio Collet, interview.jpg|thumb|right|upright|[[Caio Collet]] (''pictured in 2020'') was fastest on Day 2 of the Open Test.]]
The morning session was originally scheduled for 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. and the afternoon session was scheduled for 1 p.m. to 5 p.m. An additional special session from 12 p.m. to 1 p.m. was announced the previous evening for [[Katherine Legge]] to complete her refresher test. {{cite web|url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/04/04-28-open-test-day1-report|title=Conor Daly Leads Experienced Group Atop Day 1 of Indy Open Test|first=Paul|last=Kelly|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 28, 2026|website=Indycar}} However, overnight rain delayed the start of testing by one hour and fifteen minutes. The scheduled was revised, shifting the early session to 11:15 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. Legge's ROP session was moved to 1:15 p.m., and the afternoon session was changed to 2:15 p.m. to 5 p.m.
*'''Weather''': {{convert|63|°F|0}}; cloudy to mostly cloudy
*'''Summary''': Day two of testing began at 11:15 a.m. after track drying from overnight rain was completed. Rookie driver [[Caio Collet]] was fastest during the morning session, running a lap at {{convert|226.381|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/29/collet-tops-second-day-morning-session-at-indy-open-test|title=Collet tops second day morning session at Indy Open Test|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 29, 2026|accessdate=April 29, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} This lap would be the fastest of the day, as it was not matched during the afternoon session. [[Jack Harvey (racing driver)|Jack Harvey]] turned the fastest "no-tow" lap for the second day in a row during the morning session, running a lap at {{convert|221.112|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. {{Cite web |last=Pruett |first=Marshall |date=28 April 2026 |title=Daly leads the way on first day of IMS Open Test |url=https://racer.com/2026/04/28/daly-leads-the-way-on-first-day-of-ims-open-test |access-date=27 May 2026 |website=racer.com }}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
:From 1:15 p.m. to 2:15 p.m., the track was made available for Katherine Legge to complete her veteran refresher test. Legge successfully completed the refresher test, and was cleared to take part in further testing. {{YouTube|T_IwJooX7qQ|"Day 2 Highlights | 2026 Indianapolis 500 Open Test"|time=55}}
:From 2:15 p.m. until 5:00 p.m. the track was open to all drivers. [[Josef Newgarden]] was fastest in the afternoon session, turning a lap at {{convert|225.617|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. One significant incident occurred at 3:27 p.m. Jack Harvey suffered a major engine failure in turn four. The car billowed large amounts of smoke and briefly caught fire before Harvey brought the car to a stop in the pit lane. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/29/newgarden-fastest-as-indy-open-test-comes-to-a-close|title=Newgarden fastest as Indy Open Test comes to a close|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 29, 2026|accessdate=April 29, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} Earlier, at 2:58 p.m., a brief yellow came out after a [[fox]] ran across the track in turn one, and was narrowly missed by [[Takuma Sato]]. {{Cite web |date=29 April 2026 |title=Takuma Sato avoids collision with a fox and posts 4th-fastest lap during Indy 500 testing |url=https://apnews.com/article/indianapolis-500-testing-7174a6c30a5878501ab1865f1f8abbca |access-date=25 May 2026 |website=[[Associated Press]]}}

==Practice==
===Opening Day — Tuesday May 12===
*'''Weather''': {{convert|77|°F|0}}, Fair
*'''Summary''': The opening day of practice began at 12:00 p.m., with the track open until 6:00 p.m. Despite having handling issues early in the day, [[Álex Palou]] turned the fastest lap late in the session with a speed at {{convert|225.937|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. [[Kyle Kirkwood]] turned the fastest "no-tow" lap with a speed of {{convert|222.062|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. Palou came into the pits suddenly at 4:13 p.m. complaining of a problem in the right-rear. The car was wheeled back to the garage area where the crew partially tore it down to determine what was wrong. After short consultation, the car was wheeled back out, and Palou set his aforementioned fast lap. One notable mechanical failure occurred for [[Ed Carpenter (racing driver)|Ed Carpenter]], whose engine failed during his installation lap early in the session. The engine was changed, and Carpenter participated in running in the later part of practice. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/12/palou-take-top-spot-on-opening-day-of-indy-500-practice|title=Palou takes the top spot on opening day of Indy 500 practice|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=May 12, 2026|accessdate=May 12, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
:There were no major incidents during the session. However, there were two yellows for track inspection due to cut tires. [[Hélio Castroneves]] and [[Graham Rahal]] both suffered punctures, but both were able to return to the pits without incident. After an engine issue, Scott McLaughlin's team was preparing to install a new engine in time for Wednesday's practice session.

==Time Trials==
===Saturday, May 16===
*'''Weather''': {{convert|69|°F|0}}, Scattered thunderstorms
*'''Summary''': Persistent rain throughout the day prevented any track running on the scheduled first day of qualification. All qualifications were shifted to Sunday, and the scheduled "Fast 15" session was discarded in the revised condensed schedule. {{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/indy-500-qualifying-postponed-to-sunday-with-another-tweak-to-the-format/10821443/|title=Indy 500 qualifying postponed to Sunday with condensed format |first=Nick|last=DeGroot|date=May 16, 2026|accessdate=May 16, 2026|website=motorsport.com}}
===Sunday, May 17===
*'''Weather''': {{convert|83|°F|0}}, Fair
*'''Summary''': With the first day of qualification rained out, a revised schedule was announced for Sunday, with the scheduled "Top 15" session discarded. During the first session, which began at 12 p.m., all entries were given one guaranteed attempt. The cars qualifying in positions 1–12 in the first session advanced to the Top 12 session later in the day, while those in positions 13–33 were locked in to their positions. At 4 p.m., the Top 12 session was held, where the top six qualifiers advanced to the final Fast Six qualifying, while positions 7–12 were locked in to their positions. At 6:00 p.m., the Top Six session started, which determined the top six starting positions.
====Pre-qualifying practice====
A one-hour practice session was held before qualifying, beginning at 9:30 a.m. Only 14 entries turned laps during the session. One minor incident occurred when [[Takuma Sato]] drifted high in turn 3 and brushed the outside wall. The incident brought out a caution period, but Sato's car was not significantly damaged. [[Christian Rasmussen (racing driver)|Christian Rasmussen]] was the fastest in the session, turning a lap at {{convert|232.255|mph|kph|abbr=on}}. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/17/rasmussen-tops-practice-ahead-of-qualifying-at-indy|title=Rasmussen tops practice ahead of qualifying at Indy|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=May 17, 2026|accessdate=May 17, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
====Full field qualifying====

==Carb Day events==
===Wienie 500===
The second annual Wienie 500 was held as part of Carb Day festivities. Six [[Oscar Mayer]] [[Wienermobile]]s, each representing a different regional [[hot dog]] [[Hot dog variations|variation]], took part in a two-lap, 5-mile race from a standing start. For the 2026 event, [[Sonoran hot dog|Sonoran Dog]] was replaced by [[Corn dog|Corn Dog]]. {{Cite news |last=Goldstein |first=Steven Louis |date=May 22, 2026 |title=What is the Wienie 500? A guide to watching the hot dog race at Indianapolis Motor Speedway |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7295585/2026/05/22/oscar-mayer-wienie-race-indy-500-how-to-watch/ |access-date=May 23, 2026 |work=[[The New York Times]] |department=[[The Athletic]] |issn=0362-4331}} [[Andy Richter]] served as the "Commander in Beef" and gave the command to start engines. {{Cite news |last=Adams |first=Matt |date=May 20, 2026 |title=Andy Richter named 'Commander in Beef' of Wienie 500 |url=https://fox59.com/news/indy-500-sports/andy-richter-named-commander-in-beef-of-wienie-500/ |access-date=May 23, 2026 |work=[[WXIN]] }}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
The #01 Chicago Dog took the lead at the start and led into turn one. As the field exited turn two and started down the backstretch, Chicago Dog threw a hard block on the #05 Seattle Dog, forcing it to the inside apron. The #02 New York Dog emerged as the leader. Down the frontstretch, Chili Dog attempted a slingshot pass on the outside, but came up short. ===Pit Stop Challenge===
The 47th annual [[Pit Stop Challenge]] was scheduled for Friday May 22, after the completion of Carb Day practice. For 2026, the event once again picked up sponsorship from [[Oscar Mayer]] and was officially the "Oscar Mayer $150,000 Pit Stop Challenge" for sponsorship reasons. The bracket was expanded back to 16 participants (with no byes), up from 14 the previous two years. The eligibility for participation in the event was as follows: {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/06/02/the-racer-mailbag-june-3/2|title=The RACER Mailbag, June 3|website=Racer.com|publisher=[[Racer (magazine)|Racer Media & Marketing, Inc.]]|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=June 3, 2026|access-date=June 3, 2026}} {{Dead link|date=October 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
*Best average finish of the top five in each race's pit stop performance ([[2025 Indianapolis 500]] through the [[2026 Sonsio Grand Prix]]).
*Each full-time team is permitted to enter their highest-ranked entrant.
*Indy-only teams.
*Full-time teams may be invited to enter their next highest-ranked entrant.
The final round was scheduled to be a best-of-three series, for a $50,000 first prize. After five of the first-round matchups were completed, a light rain began to fall. The competition was halted to wait out the shower, but the rain began to fall harder, and the event was cancelled; it was the first Pit Stop Challenge to be cancelled due to rain since [[2008 Indianapolis 500|2008]]. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/22/rain-brings-early-end-to-indy-500-pit-stop-challenge|title=Rain brings early end to Indy 500 Pit Stop Challenge|first=David|last=Malsher-Lopez|date=May 22, 2026|accessdate=May 26, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}

==Race report==
*Weather {{convert|72|°F|0}}, Cloudy with occasional light rain.
===First half===
At the start of the race, [[Alexander Rossi]] moved in to the lead ahead of pole sitter [[Álex Palou]]. The two then exchanged the lead between themselves for the opening several laps. {{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/felix-rosenqvist-wins-2026-indy-500-in-closest-ever-finish/10823901/|title=Felix Rosenqvist wins 2026 Indy 500 in closest-ever finish |first=Joey|last=Barnes|date=May 24, 2026|accessdate=May 24, 2026|website=Motorsport.com}} At lap 18, the Fox broadcast did a silent lap for Kyle Busch, but it was interrupted by the first caution of the race when [[Ryan Hunter-Reay]] spun in turn two and made contact with the wall. [[Katherine Legge]] also spun to try to avoid Hunter-Reay and impacted the inside wall on the backstraight. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/24/hunter-reay-legge-first-out-at-indy-after-early-incident|title=Hunter-Reay, Legge first out at Indy after early incident|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=May 24, 2026|accessdate=May 24, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} During the yellow, most of the field made their first pit stops. Palou emerged first of those that pitted, while Rossi suffered a slow pitstop and dropped farther down in the running order. [[Rinus VeeKay]], [[Romain Grosjean]], and [[Caio Collet]] did not pit, and moved to the lead of the race. Racing resumed at lap 26, with Grosjean moving to the lead of the race. Behind them, [[Ed Carpenter (racing driver)|Ed Carpenter]] lost control of his car in turn one after being three-wide with [[Takuma Sato]], resulting in him impacting the outside wall and bringing out the race's second caution period.
Racing resumed at lap 31, with Collet briefly taking the lead before those that had not stopped at the first caution needed to pit. Palou moved back in to the lead, and began to cycle with [[Conor Daly]]. At the next round of stops, [[Felix Rosenqvist]] moved in to second place, while Palou held the lead. A few laps later, [[Scott Dixon]] passed Rosenqvist for second, and Palou and Dixon began to exchange the lead between each other, working as teammates to try to hold the lead. At lap 92, the third caution of the race came as [[Will Power]] suffered a mechanical failure and spun on the pit lane exit lane on the fluid left by the car. Power did not make contact with any walls, but was out of the race. {{cite web|url=https://speedcafe.com/indycar-news-2026-indy-500-will-power-andretti-global-issue-gearbox-failure-video-comments-reaction-details/|title='Blew up': Sudden failure ends Will Power's Indy 500|first=Simon|last=Chapman|date=May 25, 2026|accessdate=May 25, 2026|website=Speedcafe|archive-date=May 25, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260525032051/https://speedcafe.com/indycar-news-2026-indy-500-will-power-andretti-global-issue-gearbox-failure-video-comments-reaction-details/|url-status=live}} At the same time as the caution came, [[Alexander Rossi]] came to a stop on pit lane with the car briefly catching fire. Rossi was also out of the race. The entire field made pit stops during the caution period, with Dixon emerging in the lead ahead of Palou and [[Josef Newgarden]], while [[David Malukas]] was fourth. The caution period was extended due to small amounts of rain at the north end of the track. At lap 105, the [[List of red-flagged IndyCar Series races|race was briefly stopped]] to allow the rain to pass.
===Second half===
Once the rain passed, the race resumed under yellow. The green flag was shown again at lap 110, with Palou and Malukas jumping ahead of Dixon at the restart. [[Conor Daly]] moved up to third position, while Palou and Malukas exchanged the lead ahead. At lap 116, the caution came out again for a brief rain shower, but no race stoppage was needed. Racing resumed at lap 124, but almost immediately the caution was needed again, as [[Josef Newgarden]] spun in turn four and heavily impacted the outside wall. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/24/newgarden-s-indy-500-charge-ends-with-restart-crash|title=Newgarden's Indy 500 charge ends with restart crash|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=May 24, 2026|accessdate=May 24, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} During this caution, several drivers elected to pit, most notably [[Felix Rosenqvist]], [[Pato O'Ward]], and [[Marcus Armstrong]], which put them on an alternate strategy to the other leaders.
Racing resumed at lap 132, with the leaders going four-wide in to turn one passing Malukas. Conor Daly emerged as the leader, with [[Scott McLaughlin]] and Palou behind. Both McLaughlin and Palou passed Daly later and began to exchange the lead, but at lap 147 Malukas moved his way back in to the lead. Soon after, all leaders pitted, which moved Rosenqvist and O'Ward on the alternate strategy to the lead. O'Ward and Rosenqvist pitted with 36 and 34 laps to go respectively, handing the lead back to the battling Malukas and Palou, but eliminating their final pit stops while Malukas and Palou would still need to pit. Beginning at lap 175, the leading cars of the primary strategy pitted, giving the lead initially to O'Ward, but on lap 185 Rosenqvist moved in to the lead and began pulling away, while Malukas and Palou remained over 16 seconds behind after their final stops. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/05/24/rosenqvist-storms-through-last-lap-shootout-to-claim-indy-500-glory|title=Rosenqvist storms through last-lap shootout to claim Indianapolis 500 glory|first=David|last=Malsher-Lopez|date=May 24, 2026|accessdate=May 24, 2026|website=Racer}}{{Dead link|date=August 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
====Finish====
[[File:Rosenqvist passes Malukas at 2026 Indy 500.jpg|thumb|[[Felix Rosenqvist]] (no. 60) passing [[David Malukas]] on the final straightaway to take victory.]]
On lap 192, the sixth caution of the race came as [[Caio Collet]] collided with the outside wall in turn two. IndyCar officials chose to stop the race to allow Collet's car to be removed and attempt to ensure a green-flag finish. After clearing Collet's car, the race resumed under caution. On lap 196, the green flag came out again, with [[Marcus Armstrong]], who had been running third on the same strategy as Rosenqvist, moving in to the lead, while [[David Malukas]] moved in the second ahead of Rosenqvist. Behind them, [[Mick Schumacher]] drifted wide in turn one and brushed the outside wall, bringing out the race's seventh and final caution. Schumacher's car was not severely damaged and he continued driving, allowing IndyCar to clear the track quickly and have a one-lap shootout to finish. The green and white came out on lap 200, with Malukas jumping to the lead, while Rosenqvist ran around the outside of Armstrong. Rosenqvist and Armstrong remained side-by-side through the whole lap until turn four, when Rosenqvist moved ahead. Despite having been side-by-side, Rosenqvist was still able to generate a run on Malukas, and in a photo-finish Rosenqvist pushed ahead to take victory. The final margin of victory was 0.0233 seconds, setting a new record for closest finish in Indianapolis 500, surpassing the old mark of 0.043 seconds that had been set in [[1992 Indianapolis 500|1992]]. {{cite web|url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/05/05-24-500-race-lead|title=Felix Rosenqvist Wins Epic in Closest-Ever Indianapolis 500 Finish|first=Paul|last=Kelley|date=May 24, 2026|accessdate=May 24, 2026|website=IndyCar|ref=}} The race also saw 70 lead changes, setting a new record for most lead-changes in a single running of the race. For winning, Rosenqvist earned $4.34 million from a record purse of $30,906,400. {{cite web|url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/05/05-25-500-purse|title='500' Purse Soars to New Record, Increases by More than 50 Percent|date=May 25, 2026|accessdate=May 25, 2026|website=IndyCar|archive-date=May 25, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260525220422/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/05/05-25-500-purse|url-status=live}} Mick Schumacher was voted [[Indianapolis 500 Rookie of the Year|Rookie of the Year]] for his performance in the race.
====Post-race penalties====
During post race technical inspection, the car of [[Álex Palou]] was found to have violated rules regarding maximum height for wing end plates. The team maintained their finishing position in the race, but were penalized five points in both the driver's and owner's championship and also given a $10,000 fine. {{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/indycar-penalizes-alex-palou-and-ganassi-after-indy-500-inspection-failure/10824041/|title=IndyCar penalizes Alex Palou and Ganassi after Indy 500 inspection failure |first=Joey|last=Barnes|date=May 24, 2026|accessdate=May 25, 2026|website=Motorsport.com}}

==Broadcasting==
===Television===
The race was [[List of Indianapolis 500 broadcasters|televised]] live by [[IndyCar Series on Fox|Fox]] in the second year of a multi-year contract. On February 18, 2026, Fox announced their primary broadcast team for the IndyCar season, with [[Will Buxton]] serving as lead commentator and driver analysts [[James Hinchcliffe]] and [[Townsend Bell]] each returning from 2025. {{cite news|date=February 18, 2026|title=FOX Sports Opens 2026 NTT INDYCAR SERIES Season With Three Consecutive Weekends of Racing on FOX|url=https://www.foxsports.com/stories/presspass/fox-sports-opens-2026-ntt-indycar-series-season-three-consecutive-weekends-racing-fox|access-date=February 20, 2026|work=Fox Sports|archive-date=March 7, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260307233116/https://www.foxsports.com/stories/presspass/fox-sports-opens-2026-ntt-indycar-series-season-three-consecutive-weekends-racing-fox|url-status=live}}
On May 13, 2026, Indianapolis Motor Speedway president Doug Boles announced that due to an sellout of grandstand tickets, the traditional [[Blackout (broadcasting)|blackout]] in the Indianapolis media market would be lifted. Therefore, local Fox affiliate [[WXIN]] carried the live telecast; this was the sixth time the blackout had been lifted since live flag-to-flag coverage began in [[1986 Indianapolis 500|1986]]. {{Cite web |last=Brown |first=Zion |date=May 13, 2026 |title=Indy 500 grandstands sell out again, local TV blackout lifted for 2026 |url=https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/indy-500-grandstands-sell-again-123035706.html |access-date=May 14, 2026 |website=Yahoo Sports |publisher=Indianapolis Star |language=en-US}}
On May 28, Fox announced that the race broadcast drew an average audience of 6.6 million viewers, with a peak of 8.349 million viewers between 4 p.m. and 4:15 p.m.

===Radio===
The race was broadcast by the [[Indianapolis Motor Speedway Radio Network]]. The chief announcer was Mark Jaynes in his eleventh year as chief announcer, and 31st year overall with the network. [[Zach Veach]] served as the driver expert, replacing regular analyst [[Davey Hamilton]], who served as the strategist for [[Jack Harvey (racing driver)|Jack Harvey]]'s entry for [[Dreyer & Reinbold Racing]]. Mentioned on IndyCar Radio broadcast of the [[2026 Sonsio Grand Prix|Sonsio Grand Prix]] Gone was Chris Denari who retired after last years race and taking his place in turn 4 was Ryan Myrehn.
