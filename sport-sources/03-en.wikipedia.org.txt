The '''2026 US Open – Men's singles qualifying''' was a series of tennis matches that took place from 24 to 28 August 2026 (originally to end on 27 August but was rescheduled due to heavy rain) to determine the sixteen qualifiers into the main draw of the [[2026 US Open – Men's singles|men's singles tournament]]. {{cite web |title=2026 tournament schedule |url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/about/eventschedule.html |access-date=2026-07-16 |website=usopen.org}}
16 out of the 128 qualifiers who competed in this knock-out tournament secured a [[2026 US Open – Men's singles|main draw place]]. {{cite web|url=https://mytennishq.com/how-do-tennis-players-qualify-for-grand-slams/|title=How Do Tennis Players Qualify For Grand Slams?|first=Gui|last=Hadlich|access-date=2023-05-19|website=My Tennis HQ}} {{cite web|url=https://thestadiumreviews.com/blogs/info/what-is-a-qualifier-in-tennis/|title=What is a Qualifier in Tennis?|date=2022-06-02|access-date=2023-05-19|website=The Stadium Reviews}} {{cite web|url=https://tennispredict.com/how-tennis-players-qualifies-for-grand-slams/|title=How Tennis Players Qualify For Grand Slams|first=Fred|last=Simonsson|date=2023-04-24|access-date=2023-05-19|website=TennisPredict.com}} {{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/lucky-loser-explained-2023|title=Lucky Loser: Explaining The Greatest Term In Tennis|date=2023-05-06|access-date=2023-05-19|website=ATP Tour}}
This marked the final major appearance of former world No. 4 and [[2014 US Open – Men's singles|2014]] finalist [[Kei Nishikori]], who lost in the third round of qualifying to compatriot [[Rei Sakamoto]].

==Qualifying wild card challenge==
The US Open qualifying wild card challenge was a new competition featuring rising American men and women competing for a wild card in the US Open Qualifying Tournament.
The Challenge featured four men and four women competing in six total matches — men’s and women’s semifinals and finals — with the winners receiving a wild card into the US Open qualifying event.
