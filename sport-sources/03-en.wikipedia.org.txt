The '''2027 NASCAR Cup Series''' will be the 79th season for [[NASCAR]] professional stock car racing in the United States and the 56th season for the modern-era Cup Series. The preseason will feature the [[Wheatley Vodka Clash]] at [[Daytona International Speedway]], the first time back for the event since [[2021 Busch Clash|2021]], on February 13. It will be followed by the season-opening 69th running of the [[Daytona 500]] on February 21, and will end at [[Homestead–Miami Speedway]] on November 14. {{Cite web |date=August 26, 2026 |title=2027 NASCAR Cup Series Schedule - Dates, Tracks & TV |url=https://www.jayski.com/nascar-cup-series/2027-nascar-cup-series-schedule/ |access-date=August 26, 2026 |website=[[Jayski's Silly Season Site]]}}
The season will mark the first full time season without [[Kyle Busch]] starting a race since the [[2002 NASCAR Winston Cup Series|2002]] season, following his death on May 21, 2026. {{cite news |title=Kyle Busch, two-time NASCAR Cup Series champion, dies at age 41 |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/05/21/kyle-busch-two-time-nascar-cup-series-champion-dies-at-age-41/ |access-date=August 4, 2026 |publisher=NASCAR}} {{cite news |title=Family says Kyle Busch died from severe pneumonia, sepsis |url=https://www.espn.com/racing/nascar/story/_/id/48852216/family-says-kyle-busch-died-severe-pneumonia-sepsis |access-date=August 4, 2026 |publisher=ESPN}} {{cite news |title=Kyle Busch, 2-time NASCAR champion, dies at 41 after illness |url=https://www.nbcnews.com/sports/nascar/kyle-busch-hospitalized-rcna346366 |access-date=August 4, 2026 |publisher=NBC}}

===Changes===
====Team changes====
On January 16, 2026, [[Ford Motor Company|Ford]] announced their Mustang Dark Horse SC will be debuted this season for all of its teams. {{cite web |url=https://www.nascar.com/news-media/2026/01/16/ford-racing-mustang-dark-horse-sc-set-for-2027-nascar-season/ |title=Ford Racing announces Mustang Dark Horse SC will make NASCAR debut in 2027 |website=[[NASCAR]] |date=January 16, 2026 |access-date=August 12, 2026}}
On September 29, 2026, [[Brad Keselowski]] announced that he will leave the [[RFK Racing]] ownership group.
===Driver changes===
Following the death of [[Kyle Busch]] on May 21, 2026, [[Richard Childress Racing]]'s No. 8, now renumbered to the No. 33, was left without a driver. {{cite news|title=NASCAR Champion Kyle Busch Dies at 41|publisher=Wall Street Journal|url=https://www.wsj.com/sports/nascar-champion-kyle-busch-dies-at-41-34848994|access-date=August 4, 2026}} [[Austin Hill]] would take his place for that weekend's [[2026 Coca-Cola 600|Coca-Cola 600]], as was announced just hours before his passing, and later the remainder of the season. {{cite news|title=Austin Hill to continue driving No. 33 for Richard Childress Racing in 2026|publisher=Jayski|url=https://www.jayski.com/2026/06/06/austin-hill-to-continue-driving-no-33-for-richard-childress-racing-in-2026/|access-date=August 4, 2026}} Hill was announced to pilot the car in 2027 on August 24.
[[Riley Herbst]] would not return to [[23XI Racing]] for 2027, with [[Corey Heim]] stepping up to fill his slot at the team. {{cite news|title=Heim's Time Has Come: 23XI Racing Signs Corey Heim to Race Full-Time in 2027|publisher=Road & Track|url=https://www.roadandtrack.com/news/a71449773/corey-heim-signs-23xi-racing-to-race-full-time-in-2027/|access-date=August 4, 2026}} On September 3, 2026, it was confirmed Herbst would be driving for [[Legacy Motor Club]] in their newly opened third team.
[[Josh Berry]] would not return to [[Wood Brothers Racing]] for 2027, with [[Jesse Love]] stepping up to fill his slot at the team. Berry would move to LMC replacing [[John Hunter Nemechek]].
[[Brad Keselowski]] announced on September 29, 2026, that he will be leaving RFK Racing as driver and owner in 2026.
====Rookies====
{{See also|NASCAR Rookie of the Year#Cup Series}}
[[Jesse Love]] is the only expected rookie for 2027 so far, having signed a deal to race with [[Wood Brothers Racing]] in place of the outgoing [[Josh Berry]]. [[Austin Hill]] and [[Corey Heim]] will both be racing in their first full time Cup Series season in 2027, but will not be eligible for Rookie of the Year due to them both having already surpassed the 12 race limit.
