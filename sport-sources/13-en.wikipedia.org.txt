The '''2026 Boston Marathon''' was the 130th official edition of the [[Boston Marathon|annual marathon race in Boston]], Massachusetts, held on Monday, {{dts|2026.04.20|format=mdy}}. It was a [[World Athletics Label Road Races|Platinum Label]] marathon and the second of seven [[World Marathon Majors]] scheduled for 2026 following the [[2026 Tokyo Marathon]] on March 1. {{Cite web |title=Platinum Label Road Races Results |url=https://worldathletics.org/results/platinum-label-road-races |access-date=2025-11-16 |website=worldathletics.org}}
For the 2026 edition, runners who ran 4 minutes and 34 seconds faster than the qualifying time for their gender and age group were accepted to the race, for a total of 24,362 runners. {{Cite web |date=2025-09-23 |title=Boston Marathon Cutoff Time Announced: Entrants Must Have Run 4:34 Faster Than Qualifying Mark |url=https://www.runnersworld.com/news/a68009240/boston-marathon-2026-cutoff-time/ |access-date=2026-02-27 |website=Runner's World |language=en-US}} Organizers introduced a newly redesigned medal for race finishers—golden on a blue and gold ribbon—manufactured by the [[North Attleborough, Massachusetts|North Attleborough]]-based company Ashworth Awards. {{Cite web |last=Tenser |first=Phil |date=2026-02-18 |title=Boston Marathon unveils new medal for 2026 finishers |url=https://www.wcvb.com/article/boston-marathon-medal-2026-finishers/70405664 |access-date=2026-02-27 |website=WCVB |language=en}}
[[John Korir (runner, born 1996)|John Korir]] ran a course record time of 2:01:52, besting [[Geoffrey Mutai]]'s prior record of 2:03:02 from [[2011 Boston Marathon|2011]]. [[Alphonce Simbu]] and [[Benson Kipruto]] also broke the course record, with second and third place times of 2:02:47 and 2:02:50, respectively. {{Cite web |title=Boston Athletic Association: Leaderboard |url=https://boston.r.mikatiming.com/2026/?pid=leaderboard&pidp=leaderboard |access-date=2026-04-20 |website=Mika timing |language=en}} [[Jessica McClain|Jess McClain]] set a new American women's course record with a time of 2:20:49. {{Cite web |date=20 April 2026 |title=Boston Marathon 2026: All results, times and standings - full list |url=https://www.olympics.com/en/news/2026-boston-marathon-all-results-times-standings-full-list |access-date=20 April 2026 |website=Olympics.com}}
