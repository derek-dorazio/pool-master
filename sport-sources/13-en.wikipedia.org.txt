The '''2026 NTT IndyCar Series''' was the 115th official championship season of [[American open-wheel car racing|American open-wheel racing]] and the 31st season under [[IndyCar Series]] sanction. Its showcase event was the [[2026 Indianapolis 500|110th Running of the Indianapolis 500]]. The series also raced in [[Washington Street Circuit|Washington, D.C.]] for the [[Freedom 250 Grand Prix]].
[[Álex Palou]], who entered the season as defending Indianapolis 500 winner and champion, earned his fourth consecutive title, and fifth IndyCar title overall with [[Chip Ganassi Racing]], with one race to spare. This put Palou third on the all-time list behind [[A. J. Foyt]] (7) and teammate [[Scott Dixon]] (6). [[Felix Rosenqvist]] won his first Indianapolis 500 for [[Meyer Shank Racing]], beating [[David Malukas]] in the closest [[photo finish]] in the event's history, while [[Honda in motorsport|Honda]] won its twelfth Manufacturers' Cup, the second in a row.
==Background and series news==
*On September 12, 2024, IndyCar announced that the [[Firestone Tire and Rubber Company]] had signed an extension to remain as the series' sole tire supplier until at least the 2030 season. {{cite web |date=September 12, 2024 |title=Firestone extends long Term as exclusive tire supplier |url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2024/09/09-12-firestone-nics |access-date=September 12, 2024 |website=IndyCar}}
*On July 31, 2025, Fox Sports parent company [[Fox Corporation]] announced that it had acquired a one-third stake in IndyCar parent company Penske Entertainment. As part of the acquisition, Fox Sports' TV contract was extended to 2030. {{Cite web |last=Goldsmith |first=Jill |date=July 31, 2025 |title=Fox Acquires One-Third Stake In Penske Entertainment, Owner Of Indycar Series |url=https://deadline.com/2025/07/fox-penske-entertainment-indycar-indianapolis-motor-speedway-1236475246/ |access-date=August 11, 2025 |website=Deadline |language=en-US}} {{Cite web |last=Brittle |first=Cian |date=August 5, 2025 |title=Why did Fox invest in IndyCar and what happens next? |url=https://www.blackbookmotorsport.com/features/fox-indycar-penske-investment-marketing-analysis/ |access-date=August 11, 2025 |website=BlackBook Motorsport |language=en-GB |archive-date=August 5, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250805163812/https://www.blackbookmotorsport.com/features/fox-indycar-penske-investment-marketing-analysis/ |url-status=live }}
*On December 11, 2025, IndyCar announced a new three-person independent officiating board, named IndyCar Officiating Inc. Two members – [[Ray Evernham]] and Raj Nair – were voted on to the panel by IndyCar team owners, while the third member – Ronan Morgan – was appointed by the [[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile|FIA]]. {{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/indycar-to-introduce-independent-officiating-system/10784178/|title=IndyCar to introduce independent officiating system|first=Joey|last=Barnes|date=December 11, 2025|accessdate=December 11, 2025|website=Motorsport|publisher=Motorsport Network, LLC}} [[Scot Elkins]] was later announced as the managing director of officiating. {{Cite web|last=Brown|first=Zion|date=April 21, 2026|title=IndyCar names Scot Elkins to lead officiating as May approaches|url=https://www.indystar.com/story/sports/motor/2026/04/21/scot-elkins-to-lead-indycar-officiating-as-may-approaches/89714701007/|access-date=May 9, 2026|website=[[The Indianapolis Star]]|archive-date=April 22, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260422112530/https://www.indystar.com/story/sports/motor/2026/04/21/scot-elkins-to-lead-indycar-officiating-as-may-approaches/89714701007/|url-status=live}}
*On February 12, 2026, it was announced [[Chevrolet]] and [[Honda Racing Corporation USA|Honda]] had signed a multi-year contract extension which would keep them in the series when it switches to 2.4-liter twin-turbo hybrid V6 engines in 2028. Each manufacturer was announced to receive a single charter entry as part of the agreement. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/02/12/chevy-and-honda-confirm-indycar-contract-extensions|title=Chevy and Honda confirm IndyCar contract extensions|last=Pruett|first=Marshall|website=Racer|date=February 12, 2026|accessdate=February 13, 2026}}{{Dead link|date=May 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}
*On April 28, 2026, IndyCar announced a new rule that non-chartered entries would not be permitted to race outside the Indianapolis 500 beginning with the 2027 season. {{cite web|url=https://racer.com/2026/04/28/indycar-finalize-rule-limiting-non-charter-entries-to-indy-500-only-after-2026|title=IndyCar finalizes rule restricting non-charter entries to Indy 500 only after 2026|first=Marshall|last=Pruett|date=April 28, 2026|accessdate=April 29, 2026|website=Racer}} {{Dead link|date=October 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}

== Season report ==
=== Opening rounds ===
The 2026 IndyCar season started like the year before, with a pole position for [[Team Penske]]'s [[Scott McLaughlin]] at the [[Grand Prix of St. Petersburg|Firestone Grand Prix of St. Petersburg]]. {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-02-28 |title=Scott McLaughlin Starts Penske Rebound With St. Pete Pole |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/02/02-28-nics-quals-stpete |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=[[IndyCar Series]] |language=en |archive-date=March 1, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260301164026/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/02/02-28-nics-quals-stpete |url-status=live }} He led [[Andretti Global|Andretti]]'s [[Marcus Ericsson]] throughout the first stint of the race, handling an early restart after a three-car collision caused by [[Juncos Hollinger Racing]]'s [[Sting Ray Robb]] on the opening lap. McLaughlin pitted on lap 35, with Ericsson following a lap later. [[Chip Ganassi Racing]]'s defending champion [[Álex Palou]] crucially elected to stay out two laps longer to attempt an overcut. That strategy worked and it led to Palou taking the net race lead after his stop on lap 38, before cycling back to the front on lap 42. The only threat to his victory from that point on came through the final stint, where Palou ran hard tires while the others behind ran the quicker soft tires, but Palou consistently pulled away throughout the final part to win the race by almost 13 seconds. Andretti's [[Kyle Kirkwood]] looked set to take second before both McLaughlin and [[Arrow McLaren|McLaren]]'s [[Christian Lundgaard]] overtook him on lap 94. {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-03-01 |title=Champ Alex Palou Opens with Dominant March to St. Pete Win |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-01-nics-race-stpete |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=[[IndyCar Series]] |language=en}} {{Cite web |last=Barnes |first=Joey |date=2026-03-02 |title=IndyCar St. Petersburg: Palou dominates opener from McLaughlin |url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/alex-palou-wins-season-opener-with-largest-ever-margin-of-victory-in-indycar-grand-prix-of-st-peter/10801443/ |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=[[Autosport]] |language=en |archive-date=March 6, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260306202533/https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/alex-palou-wins-season-opener-with-largest-ever-margin-of-victory-in-indycar-grand-prix-of-st-peter/10801443/ |url-status=live }}
The [[2026 Good Ranchers 250|Good Ranchers 250]] marked IndyCar's return to [[2026 Good Ranchers 250|Phoenix]] for the first time since [[2018 IndyCar Series|2018]]. [[Team Penske]]'s [[David Malukas]] earned his first career pole position after beating teammate [[Josef Newgarden]] in qualifying. {{Cite web |last=Dejewski |first=Kevin |date=2026-03-06 |title=IndyCar Phoenix – Qualifying Results |url=https://www.motorsportweek.com/2026/03/06/indycar-phoenix-qualifying-results/ |access-date=2026-03-16 |website=Motorsport Week |language=en-GB}} He controlled the opening part of the race, which saw an early caution on lap ten. On the restart, JHR's [[Rinus VeeKay]] made contact with Palou while attempting to pass him, sending the points leader into the wall and out of the race. Malukas held first place until the first pit cycle, where McLaren's [[Pato O'Ward]] took over in front. [[Ed Carpenter Racing|ECR]]'s [[Christian Rasmussen (racing driver)|Christian Rasmussen]] rose up the order during the next stint, and a timely second caution allowed him to dispatch then-leader Kirkwood with fresher tires. Andretti's [[Will Power]] attacked Rasmussen for the lead and the pair made contact, causing a puncture for Power and a third caution. Newgarden was among the drivers electing to pit and quickly moved from tenth to the lead. He took the win and the points lead ahead of Kirkwood and Malukas, while Rasmussen dropped to fourteenth with a damaged car. {{cite web |url=https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-07-nics-race-phoenix |title=Josef Newgarden Hunts Down Victory, Takes Series Lead at Phoenix |date=March 7, 2026 |access-date=March 12, 2026 |website=[[IndyCar]] |archive-date=March 26, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260326201318/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-07-nics-race-phoenix |url-status=live }} {{Cite web |last=Barnes |first=Joey |date=2026-03-08 |title=Josef Newgarden earns thrilling IndyCar win at Phoenix |url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/josef-newgarden-earns-thrilling-indycar-win-at-phoenix/10803205/ |access-date=2026-03-16 |website=[[motorsport.com]] |language=en}}
The [[Grand Prix of Arlington|Java House Grand Prix of Arlington]] brought a new track as well as a new top-six single-lap shootout qualifying format, in which Ericsson took his first career pole position. {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-03-14 |title=Marcus Ericsson Romps To First Career Pole at Arlington |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-14-nics-arlington-qualifying |access-date=2026-03-27 |website=[[IndyCar Series]] |language=en |archive-date=March 19, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260319075710/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-14-nics-arlington-qualifying |url-status=live }} Palou qualified second, with the pair leading the field until they both pitted on lap 16. An error by Ericsson's pit crew saw him fall behind Palou, with O'Ward and Kirkwood slotting in behind. Lap 27 saw both Andretti cars make their second stops, with Palou and O'Ward following a lap later before Kirkwood got past Ericsson for a net second place. The pit stops promoted Power to the front, albeit on a less favorable two-stop strategy that saw him extend his second soft-tire stint until lap 45. By that point, Palou had nullified the gap to him and took over at the front. The top two took their final pit stop on lap 49, and Kirkwood, the fastest man on track at that point, reeled in Palou to claim first place with 16 laps to go. The race ended under caution after a multi-car collision, handing Kirkwood the win and with it the championship lead as Power rounded out the podium. {{Cite web |last=Barnes |first=Joey |date=2026-03-16 |title=Kyle Kirkwood beats Palou to win Arlington GP and take IndyCar points lead |url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/kyle-kirkwood-beats-palou-to-win-arlington-gp-and-take-indycar-points-lead/10805666/ |access-date=2026-03-27 |website=[[Motorsport.com|Motorsport]] |language=en}} {{Cite web |date=2026-03-15 |title=IndyCar at Arlington Results: Kyle Kirkwood Outlasts Alex Palou |url=https://www.foxsports.com/stories/motor/indycar-arlington-results-kirkwood-palou-cowboys |access-date=2026-03-27 |website=[[Fox Sports]] |language=en-US}}
Round four, the [[2026 Children's of Alabama Indy Grand Prix|Children's of Alabama Indy Grand Prix]], saw Palou take pole position in qualifying ahead of Malukas and [[Rahal Letterman Lanigan Racing|RLL]]'s [[Graham Rahal]]. {{Cite web |last=Horner |first=Scott |last2=Brown |first2=Zion |title=IndyCar starting grid at Alabama: pole position, qualifying results, start time |url=https://www.indystar.com/story/sports/motor/2026/03/28/indycar-2026-starting-lineup-qualifying-results-start-time-leaderboard-barber-birmingham-alabama/89329622007/ |access-date=April 9, 2026 |website=[[The Indianapolis Star]] |language=en-US|date=March 28, 2026}} The top three remained static after the start until Rahal took second place on lap 17. Malukas pitted two laps later, with Rahal following suit and Palou holding out on hard tires on until lap 27. Lundgaard, who also started on hard tires, rose from tenth to third in his first stint. Palou took his second pitstop on lap 45, seven seconds ahead of Rahal, who stopped one lap later. Lundgaard pitted on lap 50, overcutting Rahal to move into second place. Lundgaard cut Palou's advantage to 3.4 seconds during the penultimate stint, until the Spaniard stopped for the final time on lap 64. Lundgaard stayed out longer again and looked set to challenge Palou through an overcut, but a slow 17-second stop saw him rejoin behind Rahal. Palou, now leading comfortably, took victory to cut the championship gap to two points. Lundgaard was able to repass Rahal, who held on to third to take his first podium since August 2023. {{Cite web |last=Barnes |first=Joey |date=2026-03-29 |title=Alex Palou dominates again with 13-second IndyCar win, closes on points lead |url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/alex-palou-earns-dominant-indycar-win-at-barber/10809616/ |access-date=2026-04-09 |website=[[motorsport.com]]}} {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-03-29 |title=Alex Palou Dominates To Win in Another Barber Beatdown |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-29-nics-barber-race |access-date=2026-04-09 |website=[[IndyCar Series]] |language=en |archive-date=April 16, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260416153318/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/03/03-29-nics-barber-race |url-status=live }}
[[Meyer Shank Racing|MSR]]'s [[Felix Rosenqvist]] secured pole position in qualifying for the [[2026 Acura Grand Prix of Long Beach|Acura Grand Prix of Long Beach]], besting O'Ward by just 0.04 seconds. {{Cite web |last=Barnes |first=Joey |date=2026-04-19 |title=2026 IndyCar Long Beach starting lineup: Rosenqvist beats O'Ward to pole |url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/felix-rosenqvist-beats-pato-oward-to-long-beach-indycar-pole/10813748/ |access-date=2026-04-24 |website=[[motorsport.com]] |language=en}} Palou started third and immediately passed O'Ward for second place on lap 2. Rosenqvist controlled the pace for the first 31 laps until the leaders made their first tandem pit stops. Rosenqvist maintained his lead over Palou as they both transitioned to the alternate tires. He managed to build a three-second lead during this second stint, before a piece of debris triggered the event's only caution period on lap 57. The field pitted on lap 59, leading to a direct duel between the top two drivers' pit crews. Palou's stop took 7.3 seconds, while Rosenqvist was stationary for 8.4 seconds, leading to him losing the lead as CGR's [[Scott Dixon]] took third place. After the lap 61 restart, Palou quickly began gapping the field, while Dixon had to work hard defending from Kirkwood. Palou claimed his maiden victory at Long Beach to also move back into the championship lead, 17 points ahead of fourth-place finisher Kirkwood. {{Cite web |last=DeGroot |first=Nick |date=2026-04-20 |title=Official race results: IndyCar 2026 Grand Prix of Long Beach |url=https://www.motorsport.com/indycar/news/official-race-results-indycar-2026-grand-prix-of-long-beach/10813944/ |access-date=2026-04-24 |website=[[motorsport.com]] |language=en}} {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-04-19 |title=Quick Pit Work, Pace Help Alex Palou Run Away to Win at Long Beach |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/04/04-19-nics-longbeach-race |access-date=2026-04-24 |website=[[IndyCar Series]] |language=en |archive-date=April 20, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260420202718/https://www.indycar.com/news/2026/04/04-19-nics-longbeach-race |url-status=live }}
Palou earned his third consecutive pole position for the [[2026 Sonsio Grand Prix|Sonsio Grand Prix]] after leading every session of the event. {{Cite web |title=Alex Palou Cruises to Third Straight Sonsio Grand Prix Pole |url=https://www.foxsports.com/articles/motor/alex-palou-cruises-to-third-straight-sonsio-grand-prix-pole |date=May 9, 2026 |access-date=May 22, 2026 |website=Fox Sports |language=en-US |archive-date=May 22, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260522131513/https://www.foxsports.com/articles/motor/alex-palou-cruises-to-third-straight-sonsio-grand-prix-pole |url-status=live }} The race began with a multi-car collision in turn 1 that collected Dixon, O'Ward, Rosenqvist and [[A. J. Foyt Racing|A. J. Foyt]]'s [[Caio Collet]]. Palou and Kirkwood formed the top two during the early stages, until lap 21, when ECR's [[Alexander Rossi]] stopped on the front straight with a mechanical failure. Only a local yellow was initially thrown to allow most of the field to pit, a decision that led to criticism and a change in procedures post-race. {{Cite web |date=2026-05-12 |title=Delayed full-course caution flag prompts IndyCar rule change |url=https://www.espn.com/racing/story/_/id/48750842/delayed-full-course-caution-flag-prompts-indycar-rule-change |access-date=2026-05-22 |website=[[ESPN]]}} Palou and Kirkwood stayed out and therefore dropped back when they pitted under the belated full-course yellow. Malukas inherited the lead and held it until lap 68, when Lundgaard made a decisive pass through turns 5 and 6 to claim first place. He maintained the lead to claim his second career victory and end a 47-race winless drought as Malukas took a career-best second place and Rahal completed the podium in third. Palou recovered to fifth to extend his championship lead over Kirkwood to 27 points. {{Cite web |last=Horner |first=Scott |last2=Brown |first2=Zion |title=Indianapolis grand prix complete results: Christian Lundgaard surprises himself with win |url=https://www.indystar.com/story/sports/motor/2026/05/09/indycar-race-live-leaderboard-today-at-indianapolis-motor-speedway-sonsio-grand-prix-fox-alex-palou/90009884007/ |date=May 9, 2026 |access-date=May 22, 2026 |website=[[The Indianapolis Star]] |language=en-US |archive-date=May 11, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260511071249/https://www.indystar.com/story/sports/motor/2026/05/09/indycar-race-live-leaderboard-today-at-indianapolis-motor-speedway-sonsio-grand-prix-fox-alex-palou/90009884007/ |url-status=live }}
=== Mid-season rounds ===
Pole position for the [[2026 Indianapolis 500|110th Running of the Indianapolis 500]] went to Palou once again, ahead of Rossi and Malukas. {{Cite web |last=Nichol |first=Jake |date=2026-05-24 |title=Adjusted full starting grid for 2026 Indy 500 after disqualifications |url=https://racingnews365.com/adjusted-full-starting-grid-for-2026-indy-500-after-disqualifications |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=RacingNews365 |language=en}} Palou and Dixon controlled the early stages, trading the lead to manage fuel consumption. Rain arrived at the speedway around the halfway point, forcing a red flag on lap 105. Upon the resumption, a distinct strategy split developed; Malukas and Palou opted for a late pit stop to run at full power, while Rosenqvist and O'Ward attempted to reach the finish by conserving fuel. The tactical battle was neutralized on lap 192 when a crash for Collet caused a red flag for cleanup. Rosenqvist led the subsequent restart, but was overtaken by teammate [[Marcus Armstrong]] before a final caution on lap 197 set up a one-lap shootout. Malukas took the lead into turn 1, with Rosenqvist and Armstrong side by side behind him until turn 4, where Rosenqvist slingshotted past Malukas to win by 0.0233 seconds, the closest margin in race history. Palou, seventh, grew his lead to 32 points despite being docked five points for a technical infringement. {{Cite web |last=Marot |first=Michael |date=2026-05-24 |title=Late pass sends Felix Rosenqvist past David Malukas for the closest Indianapolis 500 win in history |url=https://apnews.com/article/indianapolis-500-indycar-palou-f462b60e9f742f38ed61ea83e1040a3b |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=[[Associated Press]] |language=en}} {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-05-24 |title=Felix Rosenqvist Wins Epic in Closest-Ever Indianapolis 500 Finish |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/05/05-24-500-race-lead |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=IndyCar.com |language=en}}
Qualifying for the [[Detroit Grand Prix (IndyCar)|Chevrolet Detroit Grand Prix]] saw Palou continue his one-lap dominance as he claimed another pole position. {{Cite web |last=Kelly |first=Paul |date=2026-05-30 |title=Alex Palou Extends Pole Streak to Three With Top Spot at Detroit |url=http://www.indycar.com/news/2026/05/05-30-nics-detroit-quals |access-date=2026-06-06 |website=[[IndyCar Series]] |language=en}} At the green flag, Palou maintained his lead while McLaughlin overtook Power for second place. He returned to second following an early caution, before taking the lead on lap 17. He lost out to McLaughlin and Lundgaard when his tires faded on lap 35, before the frontrunners pitted and Palou returned to the front through an undercut. Palou's team then opted for a green-flag pit stop on lap 63, a move which would prove crucial when a caution was triggered three laps later, forcing Kirkwood, who had inherited the lead, to pit under yellow. That promoted Palou back into the lead and Rossi to second, before the latter had to take a drive-through penalty. Three more cautions punctured the final part of the race. Kirkwood pressured Palou at each restart, but was unable to find a way past. ===Engine manufacturer standings===
The top-two finishing full-season eligible entrants for a manufacturer in each race scored points toward the engine manufacturer's championship. Points scored were the same as the driver's championship except for bonus points – a race win was worth five bonus points for a manufacturer, while a pole position was worth one bonus point (except at the Indianapolis 500). A full-season entry was eligible to score manufacturer points until it exceeded the maximum number of allowed engines per entry.
For Indianapolis 500 qualifying, the manufacturer that ran the fastest speed on Saturday received one bonus point; while the manufacturer that qualified on pole position received two bonus points.
