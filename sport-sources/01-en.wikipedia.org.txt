The '''2026 NCAA Division I Men's Tennis Championships''' were the men's [[NCAA Division I Men's Tennis Championship|tennis tournaments]], the men's singles and doubles tournament was played in the fall from November 18 to November 23, 2025, in [[Orlando, Florida]] at the [[USTA National Campus]]. {{Cite web|url=https://www.ustanationalcampus.com/en/home/events/2025-ncaa-division-i-singles-and-doubles-championships.html#tab=tickets|title=2025 NCAA Division I Singles and Doubles Championships|publisher=[[USTA National Campus]]|date=|access-date=June 3, 2026}} The men's team tournament was played in the spring from May 1 to May 17, 2026, at campus sites and [[Athens, Georgia]] at the Dan Magill Tennis Complex hosted by [[University of Georgia]]. {{Cite web|url=https://www.ncaa.com/news/tennis-men/article/2026-04-27/teams-announced-2026-ncaa-divison-i-mens-tennis-championship|title=Teams announced for 2026 NCAA Divison I men's tennis championship|publisher=NCAA|date=April 28, 2026|access-date=June 3, 2026}} It was the 80th edition of the [[NCAA Division I men's tennis championships|NCAA Division I Men's Tennis Championship]]. As part of a two-year pilot program, the men's singles and doubles championships were conducted in the fall of 2025, while the team championships were played in the spring of 2026. {{cite web|url=https://www.ncaa.com/news/tennis-men/article/2025-11-10/2025-ncaa-division-i-mens-tennis-singles-and-doubles-championships-selections |title=2025 NCAA Division I Men’s Tennis Singles and Doubles Championships Selections|date=November 10, 2025|website=NCAA.com |accessdate=June 3, 2026}}
== Men's team championship ==
There were 64 teams selected for the men's team championship, 27 of which were automatic qualifiers from each Division 1 conference. The remaining 37 teams were selected at-large. Teams played two rounds of single-elimination matches in groups of four on May 1–2 at campus sites; the winners of those regionals advanced to a super-regional round on May 8 or 9, also held at campus sites. The remaining eight teams advanced to the championship rounds in [[Athens, Georgia]] on May 14–17.

==Men's singles championship==
64 singles players qualified for the men's singles championship via competition in approved [[Intercollegiate Tennis Association|ITA]] qualifying pathway events. The tournament was played in the Fall of 2025 from November 18 to 23 in [[Orlando, Florida]].
[[Columbia Lions men's tennis|Columbia]] senior [[Michael Zheng]] won the men's singles title against [[SMU Mustangs men's tennis|SMU]] junior [[Trevor Svajda]] 6–4, 1–6, 6–3. {{cite web|last=Alcido|first=Mary|url=https://www.usta.com/en/home/stay-current/national/2025-ncaa-tennis-individual-championships-culminate-with-histori.html|title=2025 NCAA Tennis Individual Championships culminate with historic achievements|publisher=[[United States Tennis Association]]|date=November 25, 2025|accessdate=June 4, 2026}}

==Men's doubles championship==
32 doubles teams qualified for the men's doubles championship, via competition in approved [[Intercollegiate Tennis Association|ITA]] qualifying pathway events. The tournament was played at the same time as the singles championship in the Fall of 2025 from November 18 to 23 in [[ Orlando, Florida]].
Mans Dahlberg and [[Dylan Dietrich]] of [[Virginia Cavaliers men's tennis|Virginia]] beat [[Nikita Filin]] and [[Brandon Carpico]] of [[Ohio State Buckeyes men's tennis|Ohio State]] to win the men's double title. {{cite web|last=Piatos|first=Margaret|title=Dietrich and Dahlberg win doubles title as Virginia dominates NCAA Individual Championships|url=https://www.cavalierdaily.com/article/2025/11/dietrich-and-dahlberg-win-doubles-title-as-virginia-dominates-ncaa-individual-championships|publisher=[[The Cavalier Daily]]|date=November 25, 2025|access-date=June 4, 2026}}
