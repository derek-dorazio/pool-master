The '''2026 NCAA National Collegiate women's ice hockey tournament''' was a [[single-elimination tournament]] by eleven schools to help determine the national champion of women's NCAA Division I college ice hockey for the [[2025–26 NCAA Division I women's ice hockey season|2025–26 season]]. This was the fifth year the tournament features an expanded field of 11 teams. {{cite web |last=Haase |first=Nicole |date=December 16, 2021 |title=Division I Women's Hockey: NCAA approves immediate expansion of bracket; 11 teams to compete in 2022 National Championship tournament |url=https://www.uscho.com/2021/12/15/division-i-womens-hockey-ncaa-approves-immediate-expansion-of-bracket-11-teams-to-compete-in-2022-national-championship-tournament/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241226195849/https://www.uscho.com/2021/12/15/division-i-womens-hockey-ncaa-approves-immediate-expansion-of-bracket-11-teams-to-compete-in-2022-national-championship-tournament/ |archive-date=December 26, 2024 |access-date=March 1, 2024 |website=USCHO.com}} The first round and quarterfinals were played on the campuses of the top 4 seeded teams on March 12 and March 14, 2026, while the Frozen Four is set to be played on March 20 and 22, 2026 at [[Pegula Ice Arena]] in [[College Township, Pennsylvania]] with a [[Pennsylvania State University|University Park]] mailing address. {{cite web |date=October 19, 2020 |title=NCAA announces host site selections from 2022-23 to 2025-26 |url=https://www.ncaa.com/news/ncaa/article/2020-10-14/ncaa-announces-host-site-selections-2022-23-2025-26 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231203195804/https://www.ncaa.com/news/ncaa/article/2020-10-14/ncaa-announces-host-site-selections-2022-23-2025-26 |archive-date=December 3, 2023 |access-date=March 1, 2024 |website=NCAA.com}} [[Franklin Pierce Ravens women's ice hockey|Franklin Pierce]] made their first tournament appearance in school history while Penn State reached the Frozen Four for the first time ever. For the fourth consecutive year, the [[Wisconsin Badgers women's ice hockey|Wisconsin Badgers]] met the [[Ohio State Buckeyes women's ice hockey|Ohio State Buckeyes]] for the national championship.
During the National Semifinal between Penn State and Wisconsin, a new women's Frozen Four attendance record was set at 5,176. {{Cite news |date=2026-03-21 |title=Ohio State and Wisconsin each win at the women's Frozen Four to set up 4th straight title meeting |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/2026/03/20/ncaa-womens-frozen-four/732d023a-24c0-11f1-954a-6300919c9854_story.html |access-date=2026-03-21 |work=The Washington Post |language=en-US |issn=0190-8286}}

The other six teams will be selected at-large.
