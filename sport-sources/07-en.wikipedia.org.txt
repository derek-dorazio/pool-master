Aminata Gueye (born 10 July 2002) is a French professional basketball player for USK Praha of the Czech Women's Basketball League , member of the France women's national basketball team , and player for the Golden State Valkyries of the Women’s National Basketball Association (WNBA) .
Playing career
[ edit ]
Gueye began her career with USO Mondeville 's youth academy in 2017. [ 1 ] She made her Ligue Féminine de Basketball debut during the 2018–19 season. [ 2 ] On 29 June 2022, she joined Toulouse Métropole Basket for the 2022–23 season. [ 3 ]
She joined ESB Villeneuve-d'Ascq in 2023. [ 4 ] During the 2024–25 EuroCup , she helped Villeneuve-d'Ascq win their second EuroCup in team history. [ 5 ] During the round of 16 second leg against Movistar Estudiantes she recorded 18 points, 14 rebounds and three assists for a double-double. [ 6 ] During the second leg of the quarterfinals against Beşiktaş she recorded 17 points, six rebounds and five steals. [ 7 ] During the second leg of the semifinals against Sopron Basket she recorded 13 points and 12 rebounds. [ 8 ]
She joined Casademont Zaragoza during the 2025–26 season . In her first year with the team she averaged 10.3 points and 6.2 rebounds in 18 minutes per game. Along with compatriot Carla Leite , she helped Zaragoza win the Supercopa de España de Baloncesto Femenino , finish the regular season in first place and advanced to the Liga Final. She also helped the team to a third place finish at the 2026 EuroLeague Women Final Six . On 28 May 2026, she left Zaragoza. [ 9 ]
On 25 June 2026, she signed with USK Praha for the 2026–27 season. [ 10 ]
On 20 September 2026, she signed a rest-of-season hardship contract with the Golden State Valkyries. [ 11 ]
National team career
[ edit ]
On 26 February 2026, she was selected to represent France at the 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup Qualifying Tournaments . [ 12 ] She was the only French newcomer on the roster. [ 13 ] During the tournament she averaged 6.4 points, 3.6 rebounds and 0.4 assists per game. [ 14 ]
On 29 August 2026, she was selected to represent France at the 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup . [ 15 ] [ 16 ] She helped France advance to the 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup final for the first time in FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup history. [ 17 ]
