The '''2026 US Open''' was the 146th edition of [[tennis]]' [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]], and the fourth and final [[Grand Slam (tennis)|Grand Slam]] event of the year. It was held on the outdoor [[hard court]]s at the [[USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center]] in New York City. === Special events===
On August 23, 2026 to start fan week was the annual [[Arthur Ashe Kids' Day]] event, an event that aims to encourage future generations to play tennis and identify the sport's future stars. {{Cite web |last=Turcios |first=Axel |last2=Harp • • |first2=Ethan |date=2026-08-23 |title=Arthur Ashe Kids' Day launches free US Open fan week events |url=https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/local/arthur-ashe-kids-day-launches-free-us-open-fan-week-events/6539513/ |access-date=2026-09-02 |website=NBC New York |language=en-US}}
The induction ceremony for the [[International Tennis Hall of Fame]] "Class of 2026" took place on Saturday August 29, 2026, featuring Swiss former professional tennis player [[Roger Federer]] and retired producer [[Mary Carillo]] as the headliners. {{cite web|url=https://www.tennisfame.com/induction-event-information|title=Induction Ceremony|access-date=August 20, 2026|archive-date=August 21, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260821061429/https://www.tennisfame.com/induction-event-information|url-status=live}}
===Singles highlights===
In men's singles play, [[Alexander Zverev]] defeated US player [[Ben Shelton]] 6–3, 7–6 (7–2) , 5–7, 6–2, which marked the first time since 1989 the US Open men's singles was won by a German. It was Zverev's second major championship.
Four-time champion [[Novak Djokovic]] was eliminated in the first round. This was only the third time in his career that he lost in the opening round of a major. {{cite web |title=Novak Djokovic loses in opening round of US Open |url=https://apnews.com/article/novak-djokovic-mariano-navone-us-open-8541a748a9e48346ddaea528ee7a739b |website=AP News |access-date=31 August 2026 |language=en |date=31 August 2026}} [[Botic van de Zandschulp]] took 5 hours 13 minutes to defeat [[Arthur Gea]] in the 4th round. This was the fourth longest match in US Open history. {{cite web |title=By the numbers: Facts and figures from the 2026 US Open quarterfinals |url=https://www.usopen.org/amp/en_US/news/articles/2026-09-10/by_the_numbers_facts_and_figures_from_the_2026_us_open_quarterfinals.html |website=USOpen |access-date=10 September 2026}} In the match of the tournament, [[Ben Shelton]] defeated Carlos Alcaraz in a fifth-set tiebreak in men's quarterfinal action. {{cite web |title=Why the best match of the US Open finished after 3 a.m. |url=https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/09/sport/us-open-matches-late-finishes-explainer |website=CNN |access-date=10 September 2026 |language=en |date=9 September 2026}} The match didn't finish until 3:33am local time.
On the women's side of singles, [[Elena Rybakina]] defeated defending champion [[Aryna Sabalenka]] 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 to win her first US Open women's singles title. The victory was Rybakina's third Grand Slam title and her second major championship of 2026. {{Cite web |date=2026-09-12 |title=Elena Rybakina wins her first U.S. Open, besting defending champion Aryna Sabalenka - CBS News |url=https://www.cbsnews.com/news/elena-rybakina-defeats-aryna-sabalenka-wins-us-open/ |access-date=2026-09-13 |website=www.cbsnews.com |language=en-US}} A notable highlight was that she received the trophy from the Russian retired tennis player [[Maria Sharapova]]. The tournament organizers allowed Sharapova to present the title to the winner as a tribute to her own victory there [[2006 US Open (tennis)|20 years ago]]. {{cite web|url=https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4575764/stats-social-buzz-and-more-from-rybakinas-us-open-win|title=Stats, social buzz and more from Rybakina's US Open win|publisher=WTA Tennis.com|language=EN|date=September 13, 2026|access-date=September 13, 2026}}
In other singles highlights, [[Zheng Qinwen]] came from 0–5 down in a set in consecutive matches to eventually win the sets and the matches. {{cite web |title=Qinwen Zheng makes incredible comeback |url=https://sports.yahoo.com/tennis/breaking-news/article/us-open-2026-qinwen-zheng-makes-incredible-comeback-upsets-iga-swiatek-in-straight-sets-174445198.html |website=Yahoo Sports |access-date=7 September 2026 |date=7 September 2026}} All four women's quarterfinal matches were very competitive and went the three-set distance.
===Doubles highlights===
In women's doubles, [[Kateřina Siniaková]] and [[Taylor Townsend]] defeated [[Ashlyn Krueger]] and [[Robin Montgomery]] in the final and completed a team [[List of Grand Slam women's doubles champions#Career Grand Slam|Career Grand Slam]]; Siniaková's second and Townsend's first. {{cite web |title=Taylor Townsend and Katerina Siniakova Win Career Grand Slam at 2026 US Open |url=https://www.si.com/onsi/serve/news/taylor-townsend-katerina-siniakova-win-career-grand-slam-2026-us-open-01m293nna43w |website=Sports Illustrated |access-date=11 September 2026 |date=11 September 2026}} {{cite web |last1=Root |first1=Jon |title=Taylor Townsend wins US Open doubles final, praises black 'representation' |url=https://www.foxnews.com/outkick-sports/taylor-townsend-wins-us-open-doubles-final-praises-black-representation |website=Fox News |publisher=Fox News |access-date=11 September 2026 |date=11 September 2026}}
A separate mixed doubles event began on August 24 with four rounds and four seeded teams, and all but one seeded team was eliminated in the first round. {{cite web|title=US Open 2026 Mixed Doubles|url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/scores/draws/2026_XD_draw.pdf|work=[[US Open (tennis)|US Open]]|access-date=August 27, 2026|archive-date=August 26, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260826120900/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/scores/draws/2026_XD_draw.pdf|url-status=live}} {{cite web|last=Cwik|first=Chris|title=U.S. Open: Novak Djokovic, Aryna Sabalenka lose first match, fall to top-ranked mixed doubles team|url=https://sports.yahoo.com/tennis/breaking-news/article/us-open-novak-djokovic-aryna-sabalenka-lose-first-match-fall-to-top-ranked-mixed-doubles-team-161428330.html|work=[[Yahoo Sports]]|date=August 26, 2026|access-date=August 27, 2026|archive-date=August 26, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260826015928/https://sports.yahoo.com/tennis/breaking-news/article/us-open-novak-djokovic-aryna-sabalenka-lose-first-match-fall-to-top-ranked-mixed-doubles-team-161428330.html|url-status=live}} {{cite web|author=Associated Press|title=Bencic, Cobolli beat spouses Svitolina, Monfils to reach US Open mixed doubles final|url=https://www.sportsnet.ca/atp/article/bencic-cobolli-beat-spouses-svitolina-monfils-to-reach-us-open-mixed-doubles-final/|work=[[Sportsnet]]|date=August 26, 2026|access-date=August 27, 2026}} In the final, [[Karolína Muchová]] and [[Jakub Menšík]] defeated [[Belinda Bencic]] and [[Flavio Cobolli]] to win the title. {{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7542167/2026/08/27/us-open-mixed-doubles-2026-muchova-mensik/ |title= U.S. Open mixed doubles final sees Karolína Muchová and Jakub Menšík take $1M prize |publisher=[[The New York Times]]|date=27 August 2026}}
===Other highlights===
Before the US Open semifinal match between Americans [[Ben Shelton]] and [[Frances Tiafoe]]—won by the first—there were tributes and commemorations marking the 25th anniversary of the [[September 11, 2001 attacks]], featuring a musical performance and the unfurling of a giant flag on the Arthur Ashe court. {{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/articles/cvgyn32xrdpo|title=US Open pays tribute on 25th anniversary of 9/11|publisher= BBC.com|language=EN|date=September 11, 2026|access-date=September 15, 2026}} {{cite web|url=https://www.latimes.com/espanol/deportes/articulo/2026-09-11/alexander-zverev-avanza-la-final-del-us-open-y-espera-al-ganador-de-duelo-tiafoe-shelton|title=Ben Shelton somete a Tiafoe y va contra Zverev en la final del US Open|publisher=Los Angeles Times en Español|language=ES|date=September 11, 2026|access-date=September 15, 2026}}

=== Prize money ===
The 2026 US Open offered a record $108 million in total player compensation, the largest package in tennis history and an increase of 21% from 2025. {{cite web |title=2026 US Open offers $108 million in player compensation, largest package in tennis history |url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2026-08-20/2026_us_open_offers_108_million_in_player_compensation_largest_package_in_tennis_history.html |website=USOpen.org |publisher=United States Tennis Association |date=20 August 2026 |access-date=20 August 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260820171623/https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2026-08-20/2026_us_open_offers_108_million_in_player_compensation_largest_package_in_tennis_history.html |archive-date=20 August 2026 |url-status=live}}
The men's and women's singles champions each received $5,500,000, a 10% increase from the $5,000,000 awarded in 2025, while runners-up received $2,800,000. First-round singles players received $140,000, an increase of 27.3% from 2025. {{cite web |title=US Open Prize Money 2026 |url=https://www.perfect-tennis.com/prize-money/us-open/ |website=Perfect Tennis |access-date=20 August 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260820171453/https://www.perfect-tennis.com/prize-money/us-open/ |archive-date=20 August 2026 |url-status=live}}
