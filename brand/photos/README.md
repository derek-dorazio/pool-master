# Sport photos

Every photo Derek has found for email headers and other marketing, kept for later use
(`plans/email-redesign.md`). They are downscaled to 2400 pixels on the long side. These files
are not served. A photo that goes into use is cropped and copied into
`clients/poolmaster/public/email/sports/`, with a source note beside it.

All of them come from Pexels, under the Pexels licence (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free
commercial use and editing, no attribution needed. We must not suggest that a person or brand in
a photo endorses us, and Pexels gives no model or property releases. So before using any photo,
check it for logos, sponsor decals, readable numbers, named venues and recognisable faces.
Downloaded 2026-10-26. Where the Pexels number isn't known, find the photo on Pexels again and
record its link before you use it.
