# Other sites' colors

Reference for theme work: the colors of the fantasy, DFS and office pool sites our members
already know, and which of our themes sits closest to each. The aim is to feel like part of
the same world without being mistaken for any of them.

Gathered 2026-10-27. The live sites could not be opened from the cloud container that did
this, so values come from public brand-color listings and published guides. **Confidence**
says how far to trust each row:

- **official guide**: from the company's own published guide.
- **listed**: from brand-color listing sites; listings sometimes disagree.
- **estimate**: no published value found; described from the brand's well-known look.
  Check against the live site before relying on it.

## What this means for our themes

- **Sunday Prime** is safe. Navy is everywhere in sports, but navy with gold is nobody's
  signature.
- **Playbook Dark** is the closest to a big competitor: black with bright green is
  DraftKings.
- **Scorebug** reads close to ESPN (red with black); **Scorebug Navy** reads close to the
  NFL (navy with red).
- **CBS Sports** runs a product called *Office Pool Manager*, close to the wording of our
  tagline, "The ultimate office pool manager".
- Splash Sports owns the two biggest office pool sites since 2023; their colors still need a
  look at the live sites.
